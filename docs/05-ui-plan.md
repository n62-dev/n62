# UI plan: screens, menus and workflows

**Reference: the screens as built** (milestone 4a, R6–R12 and the packages after them); a point not built says so. The keys themselves are `spec/keyboard.yaml` and [06-keypad-vs-g2.md](06-keypad-vs-g2.md); the order of the open packages is [shifts/brief.md](shifts/brief.md). The plan of 2026-10-13 and the owner's review of it (section 7) are folded in; the section numbers stay, since code cites them.

## 0. In one paragraph

One interaction model for every surface above the keypad: one sheet for every list (3.1), one bar on the keypad for the place and the state (3.2), one row of soft keys for a picked history row (3.3), Mode and Settings as levels of the same sheet (3.4). Find, the unit view and the template palette are built on them. The keypad's layout does not change here (P4, P8).

## 1. What the screenshots show (2026-10-13)

The ten findings on the screenshots of 2026-10-13, all fixed (U0, R6–R8; `app/test/u0.spec.ts` and `Sheet.svelte` cite them by number); text in git at `6550cc93`.

## 2. Design rules for the screen

These sit under the three principles in CLAUDE.md and decide the details below.

1. **The keypad drives everything.** Every screen, list and dialog can be used with keys alone. Touch is a shortcut, never the only way (the G2 habit, and the fastest way on a phone held in one hand). Every sheet is in the viewport, over the history, so the keys stay live below it: a digit picks a row, ▲ ▼ move, Enter picks, Esc goes back, and a key the sheet does not use is dead and gives its feedback (3.1, R770); a hardware keyboard drives every sheet and Settings with keys alone.
2. **One way to choose.** Every choice (apps, menus, Find, templates, settings values, history actions) uses the same sheet with the same keys (section 3.1). Learn it once.
3. **State is always visible.** Angle mode, format, digits, Shift/Alpha, a running job and the current place are always on screen, in the same spot.
4. **Nothing is lost without notice.** No key goes "through" an open sheet; a dead key looks dead and gives a short haptic "no".
5. **Two keys for frequent things.** Anything an engineer does several times a day (switch DEG/RAD, copy a result, reuse a constant, change format) takes at most 2 key presses or 1 tap. The EN0 benchmark measures it.
6. **No motion that waits.** The key press is the only motion (owner, 2026-11-02), apart from the entry outline's running dash while a job runs (R155), stilled by reduced motion, and the credits sheet (R149), which rolls its text, the owner's own exception: sheets, results and marks appear at once, with no rise, fade or slide, in every setting, so `prefers-reduced-motion` has nothing else to turn off. A pressed key shows at once, in every family, in Plain keys and under High contrast.
7. **Our own look.** Our colours, shapes and icons; nothing traced from the reference (CLAUDE.md, clean room).

## 3. The interaction model

### 3.1 One sheet for every list (replaces `PickList`)

Every sheet (Apps, every Menu, Mode, Settings, Tmpl, the Shift palettes, Find, Vars, Store, Chars, the matrix sheet, Base, the stats and uncertainty sheets, the credits) opens in the viewport, over the history, rising from just above the entry line (R770; owner, 2026-11-19: every window in the viewport, otherwise it is ambiguous); never over the keys, which stay live below it and drive it. A sheet taller than the viewport (about 250 px at 360 × 740) scrolls inside its box and keeps the highlighted row in view. A key the sheet does not use (a maths key, Undo, a trackpad step) is dead: the dead double haptic, nothing typed, the sheet stays (R144's "a tap beside it closes it" ended). Esc and Back go back one level, the root closes; so does a tap on the history outside a sheet that leaves room; a sheet that fills the whole view (Mode, Settings levels, the credits, ☰ at 360 × 740) leaves no history to tap and closes by Esc, Back or its ×. With the keys folded (R53) a sheet rises from the folded bar. The table is the hardware keyboard's; no key moves (docs/03 rule 8). A pick closes it and the user goes on typing. Inside a sheet:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ▲ ▼ | Move the highlight; the list scrolls to keep it in view |
| 1…9, 0 | Pick item 1–10 directly (Shift and Alpha are ignored while a sheet is open) |
| `.`, space bar | Pick item 11 and 12, the two keys after 0 (R780): a list of at most 12 rows numbers every row, the number drawn as the key's face (`.`, `␣`); a longer list numbers 1–10 only and these two keys are dead there |
| Enter | Pick the highlighted item |
| ▶ | Open a submenu (shown with `›`) |
| ◀ or Esc | Back one level (submenus stack; the title shows the path, e.g. `Plot › Intersection`) |
| Letter keys | Type-ahead filter, for lists longer than 10 items: a tap on a key that is not a digit types its letter (no Alpha press needed); a flick down types the letter on any key, digits included. The faces and the pop show the character a tap types there, in the case it types (R820, 3.6); a key that does nothing is dimmed |
| ☰, the place, Tmpl | Close the sheet they opened (the same control opens and closes it) |

There is no hold-and-drag (P11); the sheet keys never mention it.

- **Chars (Shift + Vars, R725):** a grid of 10 columns (Greek letters, the engineer's symbols) that rises over the view like a letter sheet, so the digit keys stay uncovered; each cell shows its digit (1–9, 0) and a digit picks that cell of the row with the focus, ▲ ▼ move the focus between rows, a tap picks any cell.

- **Fill the screen (R778; owner, 2026-11-20):** a sheet with no fixed size (a list that takes letters: Find, Vars, Units, any menu over 10 rows; Chars) has a small corner button at its bottom right (a 44 px target, a drawn mark in `--muted`, no fill) that makes it fill the whole screen, the keypad's area included, so a long list shows many rows by touch. The same button, Esc or Back returns it to the viewport (Esc and Back first, then the sheet's usual step); the picked row stays in view across the switch. While it is full the keys are covered (a touch view; a hardware keyboard drives it as before). Tmpl, the Shift palettes, the Mode levels and every other fixed-size sheet show no button. The state is per open sheet: closing resets it.
- **Remembers the last pick** per sheet: reopening a menu highlights the item used last, so a repeated action is Menu + Enter (2 keys).
- **Scroll, no shrinking:** rows and grid cells keep their size; a sheet taller than its box scrolls inside it, the highlighted row kept in view, with a fade at the cut edge and a count ("7 items") in the title row. A grid has no title row: its title and × share the hint line. Shift + 6 shows the key that picks each cell in its corner (7 8 9 ÷ / 4 5 6 × / 1 2 3).
- **Disabled items** stay in place (numbers never shift) and say why on a second line, once for a run of neighbouring rows with the same reason, under the last of them ("Define a function first").
- Built once as `Sheet.svelte` plus a small model (`items`, `submenu`, `lastPick`, `filter`). Find (EN1), the template list (EN5), Base (EN6), the Units suggestions (EN2) and Settings values all use it. Find adds its preview pane and ranking but keeps the same keys.
- **Store in (R281, [store-design.md](archive/store-design.md) design A):** Store ▶ (Shift + ᴇ, the row bar) in Home or CAS puts `value ▶` on the line as before and opens "Store in", its title the value with its mark; with no value and no result it is a dead press (R702). It takes letters, so it sits in the viewport like every sheet, not over rows 3–9 as the mockup draws it: Alpha and a letter key still type a name by touch (decisions.md "Touch, keys and Store"). The letters A–Z as a grid of 7 columns (four rows: it fits 360 × 740), a filled one with its value and mark, a free one dim; then the user's longer names, newest first; it opens on the first free letter. A tap on a free letter types its name after the ▶ and presses Enter (the one store path: parser, engine, a history row). A tap on a filled name arms it (`= 5 · again?`; the line under the grid: `A = 5. Tap A again to replace it with …`); the second tap stores, any other press disarms. Keys: a key that types a letter on the line types it into the name (x, Alpha + a key, a flick down, a hardware letter), and a digit after a letter belongs to the name (`A1`; no digit picks a cell here); Enter stores the highlighted letter or the typed name, a new one too (the line under the grid says `V is free · Enter stores 4 in V · keep typing for a longer name`; a typed name that is stored asks before Enter replaces it, as a tap does: the first Enter arms it, the second stores); ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶ move; ⌫ takes a letter back, and with nothing typed closes the sheet and deletes the ▶; Esc closes it and leaves `value ▶` on the line; any other key closes it, puts the typed name on the line and does its job there (`x`, Shift + ␣ gives `x_`). A name of N62's own (`sigfig`, `pas…`) or a built-in word (`sin`, `pi`) is refused with the reason.
- **Credits (R149):** N62, then Enter, opens a sheet in the viewport like the others, titled N62, with the story of the name, the parts of THIRD_PARTY.md and the licence; its text rolls up once at about 20 px/s (a touch or ▲ ▼ stops it; under reduced motion it is a still list); Esc, Back and its ×, and a tap on the history where room is left, close it. With a stored name equal to the line's letter (N for N62) the line is a calculation.

### 3.2 The bar on the keypad

The bar (R722; owner, 2026-11-17; `views/StatusLine.svelte`) sits on the keypad's top edge, between the entry line and the keys: one line, 28 px (`--bar-h`, docs/03 rule 9), every target its full height and at least 28 × 28 px. Its controls stand in the layout lock's order (`spec/layout-lock.yaml` `bar:`, `views/barItems.ts`):

```
 ⌄ ⊞▾ [⌂ Home|∫ CAS◢]                [STD 12 ▾│RAD] ☰
   ƒ▾ [⌂ Home|∫ CAS ] [f=|∿|▦]                [DEG] ☰
```

- **Left: where you are and the way out** (R782; owner, 2026-11-20): it never shifts. `place` (APP▾, outside the track): the neutral grid icon ⊞▾ in Home and CAS, or, in an app opened through it and not in slot 2, that app's glyph ▾ with the active highlight; a tap opens the Apps sheet, a pick opens that place and leaves the slots as they were. Then the recessed track (`--bar-track`) with two segments (`role="tab"`), each a glyph and a short name (the registry's `short`, at most 4 letters; one text size, 12 px semibold) at one fixed width (54 px) and x in every place and folded, the current one filled in `--key-on` (owner, 2026-11-22, R856: nothing changes size between places, for muscle memory; ⊞▾ is 32 px and the gap after the track 4 px everywhere): Home (`tab-home`, the house, fixed: never reassigned) and slot 2 (`tab-slot`, `data-place` "cas" or an app id; CAS until chosen, `Settings.barSlot`, stored and in backups). One tap on either goes there, from an app too; a second tap on slot 2 while it is the current place opens the Apps sheet titled "Slot 2": a pick puts that place in slot 2 and opens it, Home's row is dead, the CAS row puts CAS back; ◢ marks the current slot 2 only. No hold anywhere. **The bay, after the track** (R785, R856): in an app with views, its views Symb, Plot and Num as a track of segments like the places' (`--bay-view-w`, 32 px each (a 30 px face), at every width), the current one filled in `--key-on` (3:1 on the bar); empty in Home and CAS. Busy, or the engine starting, is a ring on the current tile (Home's or CAS's tab, else the app's glyph).
- **Glyphs** (R84, the mockup `mockups/status-line.html` at `3856ad4d` section 5): every place has one glyph in the registry (`app/src/apps/registry.ts`), maths-font text or an inline SVG, at most three characters, not used by another place. The bar's tiles and the Apps sheet's rows (between number and name) draw the same glyph, so the list teaches what each means. A new app brings its glyph with its registry entry; the mockup's Statistics `x̄`, Sequence `uₙ`, Parametric `x(t)` and Polar `rθ` are examples, not decisions.
- **Right: the modes and ☰** (R783, R785; owner, 2026-11-22, R856). The modes are one outlined group before ☰ (`bar-modes`): in Home and CAS `STD 12 ▾` (`chip-format`: the format and the significant digits, `SCI 6 ▾`, `PFX 30 ▾`; Fmt's per-result FIX never shows here), a divider, then the angle as a word, `RAD` or `DEG` (`chip-angle` with `data-angle`, a fixed 44 px, 33 px under 400 px wide; spoken "Angle: radians" or "Angle: degrees"); in an app the group holds the word alone, with no divider. No icon, and neither half is ever filled: no on/off look. One tap on the word toggles RAD ↔ DEG, as before. The word and ☰ stand at the same x in Home, CAS, every app and folded. **No ⇧ α slot while the keys are up:** the Shift and Alpha keys show their state. The slot (a fixed 16 px: ⇧ or α once, underlined when locked, `a` when Alpha types lower case) is on the folded bar and, with a hardware keyboard in use (a physical key pressed, until a touch), on the unfolded one too; while it shows one it is a button, and a tap ends any Shift or Alpha, armed or locked (R726; it acts only on a pointer that went down on it, counts no Shift or Alpha tap, and an empty slot is no button; TalkBack: "{mod} on. Double-tap to turn it off"). Mode opens from `STD 12 ▾` in Home and CAS (on Format, Significant digits the next row), and from an app's ☰ › Mode, the last row of every app view's ☰ (R785). **At the right end ☰ opens the view's menu**, or the picked history row's menu; every ☰ menu has a ⚙ for Settings in its title row (Home and CAS: seven rows; 8, 9 and 0 pick nothing).
- **The guard:** a bar control acts only on a pointer that went down on it, so a touch begun on a key never acts on the bar; a click with no pointer (Enter or Space, a screen reader) acts.
- **The notes** stay off the bar: the engine's start and failure notes are at the stage's top, and a waiting update's Restart is a pill at the stage's top right. Folded, the bar and the slice are one 44 px row, the folded bar (3.5, R724). In the wide layout it heads the keypad's column (3.9).
- At 412 × 815 the history is 264 px (220 before R722) and the keys 444 px; at 360 × 740, 188 px.

Home's glyph is a house drawn in the segment's colour (`views/house.ts`, R856; the logo left the bar), on the bar and in the Apps sheet's Home row; ⊞▾ (four boxes) is the Apps button; CAS and the apps keep their glyphs.

### 3.3 A context bar of soft keys (the screen's own row of keys)

One row of up to 6 touch buttons directly above the entry line. Its content follows the context. The keypad stays the same; this is screen space. The G2 has the same kind of on-screen menu row, so it fits the muscle memory, in our own design.

| Context | Soft keys |
|---|---|
| Home/CAS, entry line has text | none, the row is hidden (no clutter while typing) |
| Home/CAS, a history row picked (▲ on an empty line, or a tap on the row) | Store ▶ · Copy › |
| Home/CAS, keypad folded (results mode, R53) | the same two, in the folded bar between the ⇧ α slot and ☰ (R724); Store ▶ keeps the keys down (its sheet's New name… brings them), Copy › leaves them down |

- **Home and CAS history rows only** (owner, 2026-10-13): Function and Solve are frozen, the Python app is removed, and the unit slots (P11) replace the prefix row, so the unit view's prefix cells are the only prefix entry (docs/04 2.7). The row appears only when a row is picked, never while typing; results mode (P16, R53) shows them in the folded bar (R724).
- Tap targets at least 44 px high; a label of at most 8 characters; an item with a submenu shows `›`.
- **☰ is on the bar only** (R722; the ☰ key of R278 retired, owner, 2026-11-17, R723): it toggles the view's menu; where a view has no menu the press is a dead one (3.7). Row 0, col 4 is **Help** (R197; Shift = Settings). **Select | Deselect** (row 2, cols 4–5, R279): Select grows the entry line's selection, Deselect walks it back; they act on the entry line only, so on a picked row or in a view without a line they are dead. **Shift + Select is Copy** (R723): what a hold on the row's result copies (Copy › 1 Full value), of the picked row, else the last finished row; with no such row, or in an app, a dead press. **Shift + Deselect is Paste** (Paste's own path, `paste.ts`; where no entry line shows, a dead press). Shift + Fmt is reserved (docs/06 3b); Shift + Vars is Chars (R725, 3.1).
- Home/CAS (R11, R276): the picked row's buttons are Store ▶ and Copy ›. ☰ has four rows (owner, 2026-11-20, R830): 1 Delete (what a swipe does, kept for TalkBack, which cannot swipe), 2 Share session, 3 Clear history, 4 Export ›; 5 to 9 and 0 are blank (a key there runs nothing and the sheet stays open). Store ▶ and Copy › stay on the picked row's bar; Paste is Shift + Deselect. Copy › opens five rows for that row: 1 Full value (what a hold copies), 2 As shown, 3 Exact, 4 LaTeX, 5 Python. Insert and Recall are not rows: Enter on a pick inserts the result and ◀ recalls the input (3.5). 4 Export › holds Work report (R130): the open view's rows as a Markdown file through the save port, a preview. CSV and LaTeX (R200) save the finished rows as n62-<date>.csv and .tex (a preview). A hold on a row opens no menu (3.5).

### 3.4 The Mode sheet and Settings

One sheet, split by how often a thing changes (owner, 2026-10-27; the picture: [archive/mockups/settings.html](archive/mockups/settings.html)):

- **Mode** (the bar's `STD 12 ▾` in Home and CAS opens it on Format, Significant digits the next row; in an app ☰ › Mode, the last row of every app view's ☰ (R785); a user key or Alt + Shift + O on Angle): Angle, Format, Significant digits, Entry, then Preset (R89) and Settings ›. Angle, Format and Entry show every value as buttons: a tap sets the value and closes the sheet; ◀ ▶ on a hardware keyboard move the mark with the sheet open; a digit (or a tap on the row's name) steps the value forward; Enter closes. Buttons stand only where they save a press (format and entry 3 → 2 taps; digits 3 → 2 through the chip's Mode); every other value keeps its ◀ ▶ row. Digits open their list (4 6 8 10 12 15 20 25 30, the current one marked) on a digit, Enter or a tap; ◀ ▶ still step by one. With no chip, Mode opens on Angle. DEG ↔ RAD: one tap on the angle word, or `STD 12 ▾`, ▲ to Angle, then DEG or RAD; 20 digits: `STD 12 ▾`, 3, then 20.
- **Settings** (Shift + Help; Mode's last row, the ⚙ in every ☰ menu's title row): the next level of the same sheet in the viewport, not a page over everything, so the keys stay live below it (Esc goes back, U1–U6 go to their rows in Your keys, any other key is dead, R770). Digits pick a section, ▶ or Enter opens it, ◀ or Esc goes back one level onto the same row, × or a tap above the sheet closes it, Android back goes back one level; the title shows the path (`Mode › Settings › Numbers`; its last three levels when deeper). A level can be a text to read (a licence, R87): it scrolls, ▲ ▼ page it, ◀ and Esc go back. Seven sections in this order, each level of settings at most seven rows of 44 px so it fits the 380 px at 372 × 767 (a level that is a list, Your keys' slots or the unit pad's cells, scrolls, as the mockup draws it; Opus pass 64): Appearance (built, R50e), Numbers (the decimal mark R57, the Fmt key cycle › with its five ticks, the decimals of Fixed, Uncertainty R40, Marks R124, Digit groups R125, Word R194: a row that opens Word size and Signed; Numbers scrolls at 360 × 740), Key vibration (with Try it; Key click, off: a short sound at each press, apart from the vibration, A66), Your keys (U1–U6 on every layer, built as in the mockup, R4b; the unit pad's cells R24d), Data (backup and restore R38a, R38b; library import and export R59; its last row says whether the data is kept, R96: "Kept on this device", "Kept, but the browser may delete it: save a backup" or "Not saved: changes are lost when the app closes", with "Last backup: <date>" under it once a backup was saved), Counters (every removal-test count: R9, R70, R89; Removal tests › Results mode R53 and › Angle chip R722, each with its own since and reset), Help and about (first-start tips R15c4, R290, R352: seven cards in this order: ⊞▾, Home and a second tap on CAS, ☰, history rows, the fold, the line, the marks, and the units key (Shift + Units, the Tmpl key; the last slot checks against a unit, or stays empty); version, source and licences R87). A section whose package has not landed is left out. A value row steps with ◀ ▶ and its 44 px buttons; ticks and switches toggle and stay; a reset that clears many values asks once: the first tap arms it, the second clears (R857); a pick in a list closes the sheet, except the theme list, which stays so the screen above shows the theme. No native `<select>`, no long-press shortcuts (owner, 2026-10-27).
- **Numbers › Decimal mark** (R57), the level's first row: Point (default) or Comma, a value row stepped by < > or a tap. With the comma the . key shows and types , (MathLive's {,}, never a list comma), the , key shows and types ; (the argument separator), and a hardware . or , types the mark. Results, Copy and Paste use the comma; list elements are separated by ;. ᴇ after the mark types no 1 (3,ᴇ3 is 3.ᴇ3), after ; it types 1ᴇ. - Mode has no key (the owner, 2026-11-17, R723; on Shift + Fmt from R85 and Shift + Deselect from R322 until then): the bar's `STD 12 ▾` and an app's ☰ › Mode (R785), a user key that holds it, ‹ from Settings and Alt + Shift + O on a desktop. ### 3.5 History rows

- A thin divider between entries (R12b; the 8 px more space is S25 part 3's); input muted and left-aligned, result right-aligned (as now).
- A plain number (a real drawn with its digit groups; R158) too wide for the row wraps at a digit-group gap onto a further line, full size and right-aligned; the mark and the sign stay with the first group, the exponent with the last. With the apostrophe or the comma only the digits before the point are grouped (R125f1), so a number with three or fewer of them (√2) has no gap and scrolls. A long input, and every other long result (an expression, a list, a matrix, a number with a unit), scrolls sideways with a fade at the edge that shows there is more; never cut without a sign.
- **A tap lights what it touches** (owner, 2026-11-20, R832; takes in R741): the first tap on a row's input lights the input alone (`data-part="input"`: the `--pick` ground and a 2 px `--focus` outline on its box), on its result the result (`"result"`), anywhere else on the row (the gap, the edge, the mark, the uses or σ line, an error's message) or ▲ from an empty line the whole row (`"row"`, as before); the reason line (R124) and Cancel keep their own taps, a running row can be picked, a note row cannot, and a swipe, a drag or a hold never picks. The next tap: on a whole-picked row the input recalls and the result inserts (R147, the G2's way), elsewhere the pick drops; on a part-lit row the lit part does its job (the input recalls, the result inserts), the other part becomes lit, elsewhere the whole row is picked. The rule is the same with the keypad folded. What is lit decides: with the result or the row lit, Store ▶, Copy ›, Shift + Select and Enter take the result and ◀ puts the input back; with the input lit the bar shows `Copy` (no ›) and Shift + Select copies its plain text (what a hold copies), Enter puts it at the caret (on an empty line: the recall), Store ▶ puts it on the line with ▶ and opens Store in (`2+3▶`); Delete (☰ 1, a swipe) and Share take the whole row. The notice says what was copied ("Copied: 0.333 333 333 333", one line) for the bar, Shift + Select and a hold. Enter on a picked row or a lit result inserts the result, and ◀ on a pick with an empty line puts its input back on the line (R720; a running row: a dead press; with text on the line ◀ moves the caret as before, R273's rule for Enter). ### 3.7 The keypad: look only

The layout stays. Only the drawing changes:

- **Legends get their own space**: every key reserves a legend line above the face; Shift legend top-right, Alpha top-left (as now), the face lower. In the top cluster the faces become shorter where they would not fit: `Home/CAS` → `Home` with the CAS key beside it, or the face shrinks to fit (one rule: never cut, never overlap).
- **The fit under the system font (R703):** Android's WebView zooms every text by the system font scale after the CSS sizes, the sizes the fit writes included, so a size read from the tokens is not what the phone draws. The fit (`fit.ts`) reads the zoom (the body's 16 px), measures what is drawn and writes back through the zoom (drawn size ÷ zoom). Floors, as drawn: a face 9 px, a legend 8 px, the bar's text 8 px, a sheet row's label 12 px and its other texts 9 px (a row's texts shrink together till the label fits). Below its floor a text ends in … and keeps its whole text for the screen reader. The bar keeps its 28 px and every width (docs/03 rule 9): `STD 12 ▾`'s text shrinks first, then each control's own (the angle word too); a place tab's name never shrinks or ends in …: where it does not fit, the tab shows its glyph only, its box unchanged (owner, 2026-11-22, R856). No key moves. The tips' Got it stays at its list's foot, which fades while it scrolls. `setTextZoom(100)` is not used: it would ignore the user's own text size (accessibility). The gate is the device test at font_scale 1.3, 1.5 and 2.0 (`tools/fontscale-device.sh`); the e2e emulation scales the tokens only.
- **Contrast at least 4.5:1** for every face and legend in every layer and every theme. A test computes it from the CSS tokens.
- **Dead keys** (today `special: none`) dim only in the layers where they do nothing. A press on one, and a press the route's one rule finds dead by the state it lands in (`route.ts` `deadPress`: Fmt with no row to take or in an app, Store ▶ with nothing to store, ◀ on a running pick, Ans in an app, Plot's line keys and commands but not its four arrows, ⌫ on an empty filter), gives a short double haptic and no other effect: no press look, no pop and no click at finger-down. A key the open sheet takes (a grid cell, Your keys, the matrix grid) keeps its feedback.
- **Key vibration is one pulse per touch, at finger-down**: a trackpad step, an armed flick and a held ⌫'s repeat make none; a key drawn dead or found dead by `deadPress` gives the double pulse alone (owner, 2026-11-02, R112; R777). The unit pad's dead presses (its cells take keys before the route, so `deadPress` does not see them) keep three: the touch's, then the double.
- **The unit view's ⌫ and More… (R118):** ⌫ on an empty slot steps back to the slot before, as on the entry line (its next ⌫ removes that slot's last token); on the first slot it is a dead key; on the empty end slot it takes the end slot off. Shift + ⌫ is dead in a slot (no cursor, nothing after the last token). More… opens Find on the dimension's units; the end slot and the result stay until a unit is picked, so Esc there leaves the view as it was.
- **The unit view's taps (R801; owner, 2026-11-20; R853, owner, 2026-11-22):** a tap on a slot's box (empty or filled; the end slot while Check is on) makes it the active slot, as ◀ ▶ would reach it, with the caret at its end; it is refused as ◀ ▶ is while the active slot cannot be left (a prefix or √ armed, a ÷ with nothing after it), and a tap on the active slot changes nothing. ← and →, two buttons left of → Check in the panel's last row (drawn as it is, each a 44 px touch area that takes nothing from the bar), make the slot before or after active as a tap on it would, the same refusal included; at the first or last slot the button is dim and its press a dead one (◀ ▶ keep walking inside a slot, R808). After any tap on the screen that opens no sheet (the field, the reading, a history row) the focus is back on the view, so the next hardware key goes to it. A hold on a slot is R797's.
- **The unit view and Android back (R700):** back does what Esc does there: out of the alternatives row if the focus is in it, else it leaves the view and the line keeps its slots; a sheet over the view (Find on units) closes first.
- **The unit view's → Check (R349; owner, 2026-11-16; its place R790, owner, 2026-11-20; R852, owner, 2026-11-22):** no end slot by default, so Enter in the last slot evaluates in the result's own unit. → Check, a button beside the reading, at the panel's last row, directly above the keys, that never moves (whatever the reading, a message or a result hold), brings the end slot (active, empty: "Check against a unit…"); it then reads ✕ Check and takes it off. A chip, More… and Tools › the end slot bring it with their unit; a picked row with an end slot opens with it on.
- **Press feedback** within one frame: the key is lit from finger-down by the app (its own `data-down` flag, set in the pad's pointerdown), not by the browser's `:active` (on a touch screen Chrome sets that from Android's tap detector: late, and stuck on one key in fast typing, F191); it stays lit while the finger is down and at least 80 ms, so a quick tap is seen (R161). Keys fire on lift because of flicks (P11), so the feedback must start on touch.
- **Key pop** (R162): at finger-down a bubble above the pressed key shows what the tap types in this layer (the Shift legend, the Alpha letter; a one-letter variable in italic, R142; while a sheet is open, what the tap types there, upright, R820), in the flick bubble's box and in the key's text colour, no fade, no motion. It stays while the finger is down and at least 80 ms, the next key's down replaces it (never two), and it never takes a touch. When a flick arms it gives way: the flick bubble (P7) shows alone. None for the trackpad, the hub, a key with an empty face or a dead key (dimmed in this layer: it types nothing). A top-row key's pop lies over the entry line, kept inside the screen. ### 3.10 Look and feel

- **Design tokens** (one file, already partly in `style.css`): colours per role, the type scale (entry 28, result 26, input 22, UI 15, legend 11), spacing on a 4 px grid, radii. Shift, Alpha, Fmt, ⌫ and the pad have the other keys' colour (`--key-ctl` is `--key-fn`); the Shift and Alpha labels are drawn in their layer's colour, an armed key fills with it (label `--on-shift` / `--on-alpha`, black or white), a locked one adds a 3 px outline. - **Shift and Alpha, armed and locked** (owner, 2026-11-17, R726): one rule for both: a tap arms the layer for one key, a second tap locks it, a third ends it; while Alpha is on, Shift stays one-shot and flips the case of one letter.

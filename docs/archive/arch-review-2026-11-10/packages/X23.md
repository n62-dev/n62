### annabell, X23: the engine's memory limit on a small phone, branch `p1/annabell-memphone`
**Lane:** by day, Sonnet, **a device** (the Fairphone) and one emulator with 2 GB of RAM. **Starts when:** X2 merged (a failed start must end, not loop). First two weeks.
**Why:** (arch review 2026-11-10, B6) The engine's heap may grow to 1 GB and never shrinks until the worker restarts (`engine/giac/build-wasm.sh:245`). The limit was checked once, by hand, on the tablet (`tests/core/ONE-TIME.md:8`); the automated test runs in Node (`tests/engine/memcap-test.mjs`). On a phone with little RAM, Android may kill the WebView's renderer before the wasm limit is reached. The app has no handler for that: no `onRenderProcessGone` under `android/app/src/main` (what Capacitor 8.5.3 does by default is not verified). Then G24's "Out of memory" path never runs and the app may close with the half-typed line.
**Read:** line numbers as of `5d91e0c4`; recheck at start. `tests/core/ONE-TIME.md`; `tests/engine/memcap-test.mjs`; `app/src/engine/dead.ts`; `android/app/src/main/java/dev/n62/calc/MainActivity.java`; `tools/README.md` (devices, the device lock, `tools/device_run.py`); `app/test-device/core.device.spec.ts` (how a device case is driven).
1. **The measurement** (a device script beside `core.device.spec.ts`, not in the nightly list): on each device, CAS `makemat(1,30000,30000)` and then `2+3`; and Home `romberg` of F187's input (`docs/backlog.md:71`). Per run: what the screen shows, whether the app's process and the renderer survived (`adb shell dumpsys meminfo dev.n62.calc`, logcat lines for `chromium` and `ActivityManager`), the peak PSS, and whether the half-typed line and the history are there after a restart.
2. **The result decides the follow-up, written as one FIX line each:** a renderer kill that closes the app: a `WebViewClient.onRenderProcessGone` that reloads the page and says so (annabell, its own package); a kill below the cap: a lower `MAXIMUM_MEMORY` for the release engine (gorge and the owner: it changes the engine's hash).

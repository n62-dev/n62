## 1. Section 4.3, the unit view's entry bullet

- **Entry (P11, P14; layout in `spec/unit-pad.yaml`):** the units key on an entry line or a picked history row (its input becomes the line) shows the same formula, read-only, in the entry line's place, with an empty unit slot after every number (a ± σ belongs to its number; a powered number's slot comes before its power; none in a power, a subscript, a name, a chip or a DMS angle); on an empty line one slot (quantity 1). The end slot "→ should be" comes with → Check (R349, a button between the formula and the reading; ✕ Check or ⌫ in it empty takes it off; a chip, More… and Tools › the end slot bring it with their unit); a picked row with one opens with it, and a formula with no number always has it. While it is open the unit pad is up: the maths rows and U1–U6 are its prefix, unit and operator cells drawn on their keys; a slot holds unit ids, never text. Enter goes to the next slot and evaluates in the last (the end slot while it is on), a flick up on it from any slot; ◀ ▶ jump between slots, never through the formula; a slot change drops a running result; Esc leaves and the line keeps its slots; a key that changes place leaves first; any other key is dead; Math opens Find on units: the units menu by category (`spec/units-menu.yaml`); ▶ on a unit row or the button under the preview puts it on the pad. Key owners (5.5): an open sheet, the pad's cells, then the unit view; a hardware key takes the keypad's path from the view's element (a digit is its prefix cell, a chord its key, Alt+F Math; letters type an open sheet's query, else a unit symbol, R313). Built on MathLive's prompts (a read-only field, a locked `\placeholder[id]{}` per slot): a slot's tokens live in R24a's model, never read back; the view writes a slot only with `setPromptValue` (5.5).

## 2. Section 4.3, logarithmic units, angles, currency

- **Logarithmic units, angles, currency:** (`rad` stays in the dimension key, and an angle is a quantity both ways: sin, cos and tan take an angle (a bare number in the mode's unit), asin, acos, atan and atan2 give one (in rad, shown without an end slot in the mode's unit); an end slot one rad apart from the result converts with the note "rad = 1 dropped" unless rpm, rev or Hz is on either side (G32's refusal stays), and rpm or rev times a dimension other than time keeps its value with the note "rpm counts turns: the speed at radius r is 2π·r·n"; currencies off, with the index test). dB, bel, Np, dBm and dBW convert by decisions.md G17; the message is `dB converts only`.

## 3. Section 4.3, the way back

- **The way back (`units/row.ts`):** each result of a unit view visit is one saved Home history row (a later result replaces it): Home's number and mark, the line with chips and filled slots, the Quantity summary (unit, dimension, σ, note) and R61's chip. The row draws the request with its units and the number with ± σ and the unit set apart (smaller, muted, upright); Copy as shown: the number, ± σ and the unit. ▲ or a tap puts the chip on the line, drawn alike, worth the number shown, with its σ (Ans is that chip); a row with no chip puts Home's number alone. A Home row computed from the chip says "unit dropped: Home has no units"; a name stored from it, or by Store ▶ in the view (Shift + ᴇ, ▶ Store by the result, Tools › Store in a name: Store in, the one store path), keeps unit and σ (6.2); with no result or a chip lacking them, Store ▶ is dead with its reason: Home reads σ, the unit view a number (R315). A Home row that reads such a name, directly or through a function, says "unit dropped: Home has no units" too (`HomeResult.unitNames`). The units key on the picked row shows its slots again (a line typed again starts with empty slots); after a result chip the slot starts with its unit.

## 6. Section 2.12, the paragraph after the rules

Every Giac path therefore sees radians. A typed or library function keeps `pasdeg` in its body, so it follows the mode at call time. The plot multiplies by the same factor. In DEG the CAS view, and a Home result that is not a number, show the result as the mode reads it: Giac evaluates the text with each `trig(u)` taken as trig of u·180/π and each inverse as π/180 times the inverse, so `diff(sin(x),x)` shows `pi/180*cos(x)` (`engine/degrees.ts`). A constant's degree quotient is reduced in the CAS view (G301): a text with no free name, no trig call and the printed form `/(pi/180)` or `*pi/pi` goes through `normal`, so `asin(1/2)` shows 30. It is slow on many roots (backlog F196). Ans and chips keep the radian text. Previews, Vars and the library export drop the factor, and a library import puts it in. Stored logs older than DATA_VERSION 3 are migrated (`engine/angleMigration.ts`). Evidence: [archive/r14-angle-mode.md](r14-angle-mode.md).

## 7. Section 2.6, the session line, Copy as Python and the mechanics

## 9. Section 3.5, the ranking order

Order (numbered as in `rank.ts`, from 2): (2) exact match of a name, symbol or alias; (3) prefix of one, shorter first; (4) word prefix inside one (`_`, space); (5) word prefix in the title or description; (6) one edit away (insertion, deletion, substitution, transposition) for queries of 4+ characters. Ties: tier 0 first, then the context's kind (variables in Vars, units in a unit slot), then the shorter name, then id. Nothing is stored.

## 15. Section 2.7, the built display of the prefix format

Built: mantissa, thin space, the prefix as `\class{pfx}{\mathrm{k}}` in the accent colour, so `1.5 m` is not metres; ENG keeps trailing zeros to n digits (`2.500 k`); PFX drops them and the point (`4.7 k`), but the σ line keeps its two digits (`9.0 G`); in Settings and the Format key cycle; copy gives the full value, never the prefix text; tests per prefix and for rounding into the next (999.96 k → 1 M at 4 digits).

## 16. Section 8.1, the web build's service worker and storage

Web build: the service worker precaches the whole build; the engine files carry their content hash in the name and the install checks it (a missing or different file fails the install and leaves the old version); a new version waits behind the old one and the update line's Restart (the only reload) activates it; `pwa/kill/sw.js` served as `sw.js` switches the worker off; the manifest `id` is `/`; `navigator.storage.persist()` is asked once; a lazy chunk missing after a deploy reloads the page once.

## 17. Section 4.2, the mark of a result with a σ

The result's mark is the worse of the value's and σ's, decided once in the evaluator and stored with the result (R271f1, `units/sigmaFacts.ts`), so the view, the Home row, Copy and the chip agree under every setting and after a relaunch; the chip of such a result keeps σ's facts, its σ cut to σ's confirmed digits.

## 18. Section 2.4's "Built in G3" paragraph and section 2.8's last bullet

**Built in G3:** stored floats keep their facts in the defs log; a result using them inherits the weakest, checked by perturbation. `evalf(e,n)`/`approx(e,n)` run at the check's precision and show n digits in Home and CAS; a nameless n (`2*10`) is evaluated once into a literal, else `⚠` without a count. Solve and analysis roots get a second polish at W+20. A value 0 in every evaluation, not exactly rational, shows `⚠` without a count, never `≈ 0` (`exp(10^-70)-1`).

- The rule that turns a result into an item's `full`, facts and chip is plain TypeScript (`state/results.ts`), so the session and the golden gate run the same code; a rune file holds state and calls, not rules.

## 19. Section 5.3, matrix and list entry; stored matrices

- **Entry:** Shift + 5 inserts a 1×1 `pmatrix` with the cursor inside; a tapped ▶ at the end of a filled cell of the last column adds a column, ▼ in a filled cell of the last row a row (MathLive's `addColumnAfter`, `addRowAfter`; a drag on the pad never grows, Tmpl's matrices keep their size), ⌫ in an empty last row or column removes it. A 3×3 needs only digits, ▶ and ▼. Shift + 8 inserts `\{□\}`: its body is one box holding the elements, separated by commas, read as a Giac list; ⌫ at the start of the body removes the braces and keeps the elements; an empty list is refused with a message.
- **Stored matrices** are Giac variables (any name, ▶ to store); the matrix editor (Shift + 4) edits one in a grid (the Num view's table code) and writes it back with `name:=[[…]]`, which the definitions log persists (6.2).

## 23. Section 8.2, the `HomeResult` bullet

- **`HomeResult`** (`engine/homeEval.ts`) carries, beside the display and timings, the facts for the mark: `facts` (`ValueFacts` in `engine/types.ts`, passed whole by the port); `full` (the copy and chip value: exact text or P digits); `defined` (what a stateful input defined); `items` and `shape` for a numeric list or matrix; `digits` (what `evalf(e,n)` asked for). `facts.why` (`PrecisionWhy`, `engine/format.ts`) says why a value is `⚠`; its `pastCap`: a literal past `MAX_EVAL_DIGITS`, compared at P and P + 20 only, not raised further (G182).

## 24. Section 2.4, the paragraph on numbers for things that do not exist (pass 255)

A number for something that does not exist (a principal value, an Abel sum) is refused by the cheapest needed check (the term's limit, ∫|f| near a singularity); a check Giac's timeout stops leaves `⚠`. An integral that holds a name besides its variable (an inner integral, or a free name) is checked the same way with each enclosing variable at two generic interior points of its interval (never the midpoint: its divergence at either point refuses) and each free name at four values (1.38 and −2.72, then the same two scaled by 1 + |a| + |b| beyond the interval's ends; a value counts as diverging when either of the two points of the enclosing variables diverges), refused only when all four diverge (G771); a nested integral's two-number answer is `⚠`, never a list. Giac's closed form of a series to ∞ whose term is rational in k, or (−1)^k times one, is checked against the series' own terms (partial sums plus an Euler–Maclaurin or Euler tail at two cuts, G618): a closed form they contradict, or an infinity for such a series, is refused.

## 25. Section 2.6, Paste of a copied name (pass 255)

a copied name does not (G784, decisions.md): the text a Copy, Copy as shown, Copy exact or a hold last wrote from a row with a name (`2*i` from the name i) is kept across a relaunch, cleared by the next copy without one, and refused by Paste as a name, not a number (G784f1), never read as the constant i; a product of names is refused.

## 26. Section 2.8, the result chip's one-term rule (pass 255)

One term: a × (`\times`, the × key's) goes between it and a number, a closing bracket or a chip before the cursor, or a number, an opening bracket or a chip after it (`entry/oneTerm.ts`), and a leading minus or a top-level sum is bracketed: `2` then −5/6 is `2\times\left(-\frac{5}{6}\right)`, never 2⅚.

## 27. Section 4.2, the uncertainty formula and its notes (pass 256)

σ² = Σᵢ (∂f/∂xᵢ)² σᵢ² + Σᵢ Σⱼ [½ (∂²f/∂xᵢ∂xⱼ)² + ∂f/∂xᵢ · ∂³f/∂xᵢ∂xⱼ²] σᵢ² σⱼ², GUM 5.1.2 with its note, Giac's `diff` at the inputs' values; σ carries ⚠ "first order does not hold here" when the second-order part is more than 10 % of σ², "the interval reaches a singular point" when an input's x ± 2σ reaches a point where ∂f/∂x is not defined, and "the interval reaches a jump" when it reaches a step function's jump; no Monte Carlo check (GUM Supplement 1).

## 28. Section 5.5, the messages of a number after a mark (pass 256)

`input/parse.ts` `markThen` throws when a number follows the mark (`30°15`, `1°2′3`, `30°15′36″2`, `2%3`, `5!3`), and the message names the mark it lacks (`parse.numberAfterDegree`: ′ for minutes; `parse.numberAfterPrime`: ″ for seconds; `parse.numberAfterMark`: × for a product), as `parse.oneExponent` does for ᴇ.

## 29. Section 4.3, a chip in one ratio unit (pass 258)

A chip in one ratio unit (%, ‰, ppm, ppb) enters Home and CAS as its pure number (G782), with no such line; in the unit view its slot starts with that unit, so its factor is kept and G369 drops it beside another unit (0.42 % × 200 m = 0.84 m), and Store ▶ on it stores the pure number with its σ, while a rev chip's slot still starts empty (G782f1).

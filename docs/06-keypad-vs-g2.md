# Keypad against the G2: every difference and why

Written 2026-10-13 from a key-by-key comparison of the G2 keypad with `spec/keyboard.yaml`, checked against the decision docs, and then reviewed point by point with the owner (decision P12 in [decisions.md](decisions.md)). **One place for this question:** when a key differs from the G2, the reason is here; the layout itself is `spec/keyboard.yaml`, the G2 behaviour is `spec/mapping/vc-2026-10-11/keys.yaml`. The Fmt key (our replacement of the On/Off key) is left out. Rules that decide a difference: [03-feature-analysis.md](03-feature-analysis.md) section 0 (rules 5–9, 11) and CLAUDE.md.

## 3. Decided by the owner on 2026-10-13 (P12)
1. **a/b becomes a toggle for the form of the last result**: decimal ↔ fraction, polar ↔ rectangular (real + imaginary), and later forms of the same kind. The fraction template stays on ÷ and in Tmpl. The Fmt key keeps the number formats (Std, Sci, Eng, Fix, Pfx); its cycle has no fraction, mixed or polar entries. Built in R10 (`f296a92`).
2. **No Home or CAS key; the bar is the switch.** The App key (Home/CAS) and the CAS key go; no key shows the current mode. Built in R15c1 and R15c2 (the status line at the screen's top: its place opened Apps, Home and CAS its first rows, 2 taps), then R722 (owner, 2026-11-17): the bar sits on the keypad's top edge, Home and CAS are its tabs (1 tap each, from an app too) and Apps ▾ its left end; the G2's Home and CAS cells hold user keys, empty by default; Mode is a tap on a chip, Settings the last row of every ☰ menu. It rewrites rule 9's wording "switching lives on the keys, not in bars" (docs/03).
3. **Same for the other view keys, and a cleaner top section.** Symb, Plot and Num are folded into the bar the same way if R15a confirms it (every view stays one action away); the arrow pad moves one line down; the top cluster (menu keys, Esc, Cancel, View, Help) is tidied so it takes fewer rows. Built as R3 (R15c, R15c2) and R146: Symb, Plot and Num are tabs, Menu is ☰, Esc took Cancel's job; the rows 0–2 hold U1–U6, the round pad, and (R146, R278, then R723) Help | Esc, Undo | Redo, Select | Deselect, with Settings | Clear, ⇤ | ⇥ and Copy | Paste on Shift (owner, 2026-11-06, 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-17); results mode is R53's handle and swipe, not a key. Rules 6 and 8 allow this only as the owner's one-off decision for the top cluster, before the layout is learned; the maths keys below do not move, and after R15c the cluster is fixed too.
4. **The exponent key types an E mark instead of the `×10^` template.** The template needs space, hurts the formula's readability and caused the silent wrong exponent of M4-2 (`2.2E-9 × 4.7E3`). The key becomes `ᴇ` (the mark the G2 also draws), is no longer called EEX or ×10ⁿ, and its legend is `ᴇ`. Results keep the E notation of the Sci and Eng formats (`1.5E-9`). Built in R14: the key (id `emark`) inserts `\emark`, drawn ᴇ as one token; on an empty line or after an operator it types 1ᴇ as the G2 does; ÷ and the power keys take the whole number.
5. **Shift + x = y stays** (section 2).
6. **Menus are revamped in the step that adds the next menus** (the Tmpl palette and the form toggle of R10): done as S2's seven rules ([archive/r8-menus.md](archive/r8-menus.md), decisions.md "S2: one menu system"); Mode is on the status chips, R10's Tmpl palette stays as built.
7. **Alpha + 0 (the quote mark) is removed.** Python is gone, strings have no use in Home or CAS, and an unmatched `"` is an error waiting to happen. A key that does nothing in a layer is dimmed (R6). Removal test: it comes back (as a pair) when a feature needs text. Built in R13.
8. **Shift + ◀ and Shift + ▶ (line start and end) were removed** in R13 and came back by their removal test as ⇤ ⇥ (R15c, built in R15c2; since R146 Shift + Undo and Shift + Redo), the box's start and end were on Shift + Copy and Shift + Paste until R275 removed them (the keys went in R278); ◀ ▶ keep no Shift layer.
9. **Info, Notes and User stay omitted** (section 2).
10. **The G2's Shift palettes come back** (owner's answers to R10): Shift + 6 relations and logic, Shift + 9 symbols and Greek with `!`. Package R18.

## 3b. The layout at the v0.1.0 tag and how it grows (owner, 2026-11-12)
The owner's rule: "the layout should stay the same from start of release". From the tag on, nothing that works moves. A key layer, a gesture or a numbered row that does something at the tag keeps doing it. A cell that is blank at the tag may be filled later, because nobody has learned it. **In one line: freeze what works, reserve what is blank, grow only into blank space.** A test holds it: `spec/layout-lock.yaml` pins every live key layer, every reserved cell and the fixed rows of each numbered list: ☰ in Home and CAS, Mode, Settings, Apps, Base, the matrix sheet, the Function and Solve views' ☰, the stat sheets, the unit menu's categories and its cell sheet (U1 to U6), the three palettes (Tmpl, Shift + 6, Shift + 9), and the modifiers (`modifiers:`: what a Shift or Alpha tap, second tap, third tap and flick leave, and the bar's sign, as a fresh Session does them, R726); the desktop chords (R131) and rows of stored content are not in it. A new file that opens a numbered list must be in `LIST_BUILDERS` (`layoutLock.ts`), or the test fails; and `app/src/keyboard/layoutLock.test.ts` (in the gate) compares it with the spec and with the lists as the code builds them. A live line is never edited or deleted; growth into blank space is a new or filled line, and a move is a line under `moves:` that names the old and new value, the version and the decisions.md entry with the owner's yes. The test also reads the last release tag's lock (`vX.Y.Z`; a pre-release such as a beta pins nothing) and fails if a line live there is missing or edited.

**Rows 0–2 at the tag** (the maths rows below them do not change):

| Row | Cols 0–1 | Cols 2–3 | Col 4: tap, Shift | Col 5: tap, Shift |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | U1, U2 | ▲ | Help, Settings | Esc, Clear |
| 1 | U3, U4 | ◀, ▶ | Undo, ⇤ | Redo, ⇥ |
| 2 | U5, U6 | ▼ | Select, Copy | Deselect, Paste |

**The three cells decided with the rule**
- **Row 0, col 4: Help; Shift: Settings** (owner, 2026-11-17, R723). Help shows the function at the cursor, else Find (R197). The cell held ☰, a Menu key (R278), until the bar with its ☰ came onto the keys (R722); a second ☰ a row lower saved no reach, so the cell went back to Help, the G2's function in this column, under its own id `help` (rule 3). ☰ stays on the bar, and Alt + M on a desktop.
- **Shift + Select is Copy and Shift + Deselect is Paste; Mode has no key** (owner, 2026-11-17, R723). Copy takes what a hold on the row's result copies, of the picked row, else the last finished row; Paste is ☰ → Paste's path. Mode opens from the STD and 12 chips on the keys (R722), a user key that holds it, ‹ from Settings and Alt + Shift + O. Shift + Fmt (Mode's from R85) is reserved; Shift + Vars is Chars (R725; Paste's from R329 until R723).
- **Folding (results mode) is the swipe or a tap on ⌄; ⌃, the bar, a swipe up, Back, Esc and the desktop key bring the keys back** (R724, owner 2026-11-17: ⌄ on the entry field's corner, and the folded bar). There is no Fold key and no ☰ row, and the handle on the keypad's top edge is gone (R283). A first-start tip teaches ⌄ and the swipe (R290), a hardware keyboard gets a key for it (R131), and a user key may hold it after the tag. The side-by-side layout of a tablet or a desktop has no fold, because the keypad covers no history there (R224).

**A reserved cell** is blank on purpose, so that filling it later is not a move:
1. It has no legend in that layer, and the key is drawn dimmed in that layer only.
2. A press does nothing but count: the dead key's double pulse (docs/05 3.7), no key pop, ✕ in the flick bubble, and one count under the key's id and layer (R127). The counts show whether people reach for the cell.
3. The key's id is stable and names what the key does. An id is never reused for another function: `help` left row 0, col 4 together with Help (R278) and came back with it (R723), and `menu` retired with the ☰ key.
4. TalkBack reads no such layer.
5. It is not a user slot. If a user could bind it, a later built-in would take that binding away, which is a move for that user. The user's keys are U1–U6.
6. It is filled only with the owner's yes, from the counts of the task benchmark (docs/04 5.1), and the layout lock records the fill as an addition.

**Reserved at the tag:** the Shift layers of a b/c (for the ° ′ ″ form, mh-E9; owner, 2026-11-17), 7, and Fmt (Fmt's tap stays as it is; owner, 2026-11-17). (Shift + Vars was reserved for Chars until R725 filled it, owner, 2026-11-17; Paste moved to Shift + Deselect, R723; recorded in the layout lock as a fill.) (Shift + `( )` and Shift + 0 were reserved until the owner filled them on 2026-11-14, R323: ∥ and %; Shift + 1 on 2026-11-15, R327: σ, the uncertainty sheet of a result with σ; each recorded in the layout lock as an addition.) (Shift + 7 is the G2's List; it waits for the list editor that comes with Statistics.) No tap is blank: U1–U6 are empty, but they are the user's. Closed, not reserved: the pad has no Shift layer (section 3 point 8) and Alpha + 0 is dead (section 3 point 7). A planned layer that misses the tag stays dead and dimmed until its package lands, which is a fill and not a move (Shift + 4, R187, and Shift + −, R193, landed before it).

**The Alpha layers that fall back are final.** The keys of rows 0–2 other than U1–U6, and Enter, ⌫, Fmt, `.` and ␣, have no letter: a flick down on them does the tap. They get no letter later, because a letter there would change what a flick down does.

**K1 is intended behaviour.** A pending one-shot Shift is used up by the first step of a drag on the pad. Shift is one-shot and the next press uses it (`spec/keyboard.yaml`, the gestures); a drag step is a press. Nothing changes.

**The growth rule: where a later feature attaches**
1. **Keys:** a reserved cell, or a layer that is dead in its own context only (a mode or a slot), as the unit pad does (docs/03 rule 8). Never a layer that is live in that context.
2. **Numbered lists** (☰ Home/CAS, Mode's rows, Settings' sections, Apps, Tmpl, the Shift palettes, the matrix sheet, Base): a fixed row keeps its digit (docs/05 3.1). A new fixed row is appended after the last fixed row, or goes into a submenu. Rows of stored content (names, matrices) come after the fixed rows, because their order changes anyway. So the exports share one row, Export ›, as item 7 of ☰ Home/CAS (R181, R200, R130; 8, 9 and 0 are blank, R720), and the matrix sheet has its functions at 1 to 8, New at 9 and the stored matrices after them (R187, R188, R190).
3. **A feature of one history row:** a further button on the picked row's bar, after Store ▶ and Copy › (docs/05 3.3 allows six). Never the row's tap, second tap, hold or swipe.
4. **Gestures:** a new gesture only on a surface that has none, on top of its tap (docs/03 rule 6). The flick on the place name (section 4) would be one.
5. **Sheets, Find, Settings rows and the places a user key can hold:** always free to grow.
6. **Hardware keys:** every new key layer brings its desktop key (R131's spec test).

**Removal tests after the tag** (docs/03 rule 11). A removal test, or a "would change if", on a key, a layer, a gesture or a numbered row still counts after the tag. It acts only at a version boundary, with the owner's yes. A removal only blanks the cell, which becomes reserved; the cell is never re-filled in the same version. This holds for the open clauses already written down: Shift + x (section 2), the Alpha key (docs/04 5.1), and the letters, the 36 px rows and results mode (decisions.md).

## 5. About the comparison file this was built from
An outside review produced a YAML list of differences (`gemini-code-1791904688276.yaml`, not kept in the repo). Read it with two caveats:
1. **It lists planned keys as removed or omitted.** Help, Matrix and `[ ]` are planned (section 1); the Math and Tmpl keys, Memory, Units, `{ }` (R54) and Base (R193) are built; none was dropped. Two small errors: it says the clone's `(−)` replaced a `+/−` toggle (the G2 key does not toggle), and that Alpha + 0 inserts a single quote mark (it types one double-quote character; now removed).
2. **It misses differences that are already documented:** ☰'s contents (Home/CAS actions: copy, paste, share, clear history, P8), Paste (☰ 4: a number from the clipboard, where the G2 lists past entries; docs/04 2.6), and the unit-slot overrides (µ on `(−)`, Ω on the comma, ° on a b/c, flick letters: S1's, replaced by the unit pad in R24c; docs/archive/r7-unit-slots.md).

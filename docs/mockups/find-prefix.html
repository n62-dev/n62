Find and the prefix keys: how to make them stand out

From the owner's list of 2026-11-22: point 12 (Find gets lost in the unit view) and point 9 (the unit view's prefix keys differ only by colour), plus small strips for 19 (Settings on the tap, Help on Shift) and 16 (which letters are italic). Every keypad below is 360 px wide, the narrowest phone, in the app's own colours.

Point 12: Find

Find sits on the same key in both views, and for the same reason: it is the tap of the key in the G2's Math position (row 3, column 2), and the unit pad keeps every key where it is, so your thumb knows one Find key. Today nothing marks it. In the unit view it even loses its ▾. The unit pad draws that key as a pad cell, and a pad cell never carries the mark, so this is a bug of its own. Enter stays the only key filled with the accent colour. A ring around a key is out too, because R729 (queued) makes a ring mean "armed".

Point 9: the prefix keys in the unit view

Measured on the running app, a prefix key's colour differs from the unit key beside it by only 1.03 to 1.41 : 1, depending on the theme. In N62 Dark it is 1.11 : 1 (#1e4948 against #194241), so the prefix m and the metre m look almost the same. The usual minimum for telling two controls apart is 3 : 1. Only High contrast adds an outline today. A shape cue works in every theme, in Plain keys and for colour-blind eyes.

Point 19: Settings on the tap, Help on Shift

As the tap's face, the word "Settings" is shrunk to 10.7 px to fit the 55 px key at 360 px (measured). That is smaller than the 11 px legends and every other face. The ⚙ is already Settings' sign in every sheet's title row.

Point 16: which letters are italic

The rule (R142): a legend is italic exactly when its key types one letter, because that letter is a variable. Shift and Alpha type nothing, so their words stay upright, like Help or Fmt. The one place where the keypad breaks the spirit of the rule is the letter that stands for "your number" in a template (x², xʸ, ⁿ√, |x|, x⁻¹, eˣ, 10ˣ, a b/c). It is upright, while the x key right beside it draws an italic x.

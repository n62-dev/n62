The uncertainty sheet: the budget, U, relative σ and the short form

2026-11-14, package R220 (mh-C1, mh-C2; must-have.md decisions 1 and 2). Nothing here is built. One sheet that says, for a result with σ, its short form, its standard uncertainty (σ), U = 2σ with k = 2, its relative σ, and each input's share of σ². The rows follow G272's rules (the model's package; once it merges its file goes). Every number is derived from the inputs shown (GUM 5.1.2 with its second-order note, the way app/src/units/evaluate.ts computes σ); sizes are drawn. Phones at 360 px wide, in the N62 family.

1 · Where it opens

From a σ line that is already on the screen; no new key (docs/03 rule 8). Home: a picked row whose σ line shows (Settings › Numbers › Uncertainty: σ on tap, R40); a tap on that σ line opens the sheet. The unit view: a tap on the result's σ line. The dashed orange outline marks the tap target (≥ 44 px high with its padding, drawn). The sheet rises over the keypad's maths rows like every pick sheet (docs/05 3.1); the top cluster stays uncovered, Esc or a tap on a cluster key closes it. Its title is the result it explains, with its mark.

2 · A or B: how a row reads when the second-order term matters

A (recommended; the owner said yes to it for G272 on 2026-11-12, shown again here on the screen): every row is a share of σ², the second-order term is one signed row, and the rows add to the σ² the head shows. A row can read above 100 % when the second-order term is negative. B: the input rows are shares of the first-order sum a only, so they never pass 100 %; the second-order term is a separate line under them, and the rows no longer add to the σ² that is shown.

3 · The states (variant A)

Each block carries its data-state name. Row 1 is highlighted (▲ ▼ move it). An input row is the input as typed or the constant's name; its uᵢ is its part of σ in the result's unit (|∂f/∂xᵢ|·uᵢ, 2 significant digits); the second-order row has none. Rows are sorted by share, the second-order row last; a row whose model says independent carries a note in the warn colour.

The pick

Needs the user (R220). 1. The rows: A (recommended: the rows add to the σ² the head shows; the owner's yes of 2026-11-12 for G272 stands unless this page changes it) · B (first-order rows over a, never above 100 %, second order as a line). 2. How keys open the sheet: a tap on the σ line is drawn; a key path is needed (docs/05 rule 1) and adds no key. Recommended: an Uncertainty item in the ☰ menu's title row beside ⚙, shown only on a row with σ, so the ten numbered items keep their numbers. Or: Enter on the picked row's σ line, once ▼ can move the pick onto it.

### gorge, G508: `max`, `min` and `mod` in a unit formula, with ± too, branch `p1/gorge-unitmaxmod`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Opus (σ through a function whose derivative jumps: a wrong σ is shown as confirmed digits). **Starts when:** after the v0.1.0 tag (0.2). Before G518, which reuses the rewrite.

**Why:**
- **The owner's list** (night review 2026-11-09, be-9's residual, archived in `docs/archive/night-review-2026-11-09.md:73`; carried in 11-14 and 11-16): "`max` and `min` of ± values and `mod` in a unit formula are refused with the honest generic line, a feature gap". A tolerance check (the larger of two clearances, a length modulo a pitch) needs them.
- **Probed at `5a583f31`** (`tools/probes/unit-result.ts`, Find index of phase-1):
  - `max(3*{m},4*{m})` = 4 m (works without ±);
  - `max(pasupm(3,0.1)*{m},pasupm(4,0.2)*{m})`, `min(pasupm(3,0.1)*{m},4*{m})` and `max(pasupm(3,0.1),pasupm(4,0.2))`: "The unit view could not evaluate this". The detail is `GIAC_ERROR: Ifte: Unable to check test`, from the σ program `pasuu` (`app/src/units/evaluate.ts:166`, called at `:421`). The cause: Giac leaves `diff(max(x,4),x)` unevaluated (smoke), so the derivative never becomes a number.
  - `pasmod(7*{m},3*{m})` and `pasmod(pasupm(7,0.1),3)` (what `7\bmod3` and Find's mod give: `app/src/input/emit.ts:79`, `engine/ourNames.ts:12`) are refused by `buildJob`'s guard "a reserved name in the formula" (`app/src/units/build.ts:642`): `pasmod` starts with `pas` and is not in `ourSymbol` (`:17`). Our own definition (`engine/homeEval.ts:161-163`) never runs.
- **The rewrite is exact and differentiable away from ties:** `max(a,b) = (a+b+|a−b|)/2`, `min(a,b) = (a+b−|a−b|)/2`. Giac gives `diff((a+b+abs(a-b))/2,a)` = `(1+abs(a-b)/(a-b))/2`, at a = 3, b = 4 the ∂/∂b is 1, and `singular(abs(a-4)/(a-4),a)` = `[4]` (smoke). So a tie within 2σ meets G41's existing "the interval reaches a singular point" ⚠. `pasmod`'s `a − |b|·floor(a/|b|)` has ∂/∂a = 1 (smoke), and its floor jump meets G143's "the interval reaches a jump".

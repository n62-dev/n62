**Lane:** B, the weekly extreme night (its first run is this package's), Sonnet (a report and a counter; it changes no mark). **Starts when:** after the v0.1.0 tag (0.3). Before G521 (X10, the range of `≈`), which reads its misses.

**Why:**
- **Nobody can say whether the checking code converges** (the arch review of 2026-11-10, M1): `fix` commits on `app/src/engine` ran at 77, 52, 40 and 72 a week from 10-14 to 11-10; fixes for two rules that clash did not fall (11, 7, 4, 14).
- **The sweep finds misses but keeps no rate.** `node scripts/sweep.ts --nightly` (`app/scripts/sweep.ts:6-15`, as of `5a583f31`) writes a report of failures not in `tests/golden/sweep-known.yaml` or `sweep-nightly-known.yaml` to `.tasks/`, which is gitignored; there is no tracked line per run, no count per 10,000 trees, and no record of the shape of the inputs that missed.

1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1** (`app/scripts/sweep.test.ts`; the functions do not exist yet):
   - `treeFeatures(tree)` gives four numbers read before the tree runs: the digits of its longest literal, the largest absolute power-of-ten exponent written in it, its depth of nested calls, its node count. A fixed-seed fixture of 20 trees from `gen.ts` pins them (`source: derived`, counted by hand for 3 of them in the test's comment);
   - `rateLine(summary)` gives one JSON line: date, commit, engine hash, seed, family, trees judged, false marks (a `≈` or `=` with a wrong digit, or a wrong `⚠ n`), each split into **plain** (literal ≤ 30 digits, exponent ≤ 30, depth ≤ 4, nodes ≤ 200: the owner's floor) and **extreme** (the rest); known rows are counted (`known: n`), never hidden;
   - `missLines(summary)` gives one line per false mark with its four features, the check, the input and the mark shown.
2. **The change:**
   - the week's 10,000 trees are two families on one seed: 5,000 of `gen.ts` (`--nightly --seed <clock> --count 5000 --part i/4`) and 5,000 of `edges.ts` (the weekly night's `--nightly --edges --seed <clock> --count 5000 --part i/4`, already run), each part under `tools/devlock.sh slice` as the nightly run is; the parts keep the count judged beside their failures;
   - `--rate` is a flag of the shrink step (`--shrink <seed> --count 5000 [--edges] --rate`): after the report it appends one line per family (`family: "gen" | "edges"`) to `tests/golden/mark-rate.jsonl` and that family's misses to `tests/golden/mark-rate-misses.jsonl`; a false mark is a judge row of kind `wrong` (`judge.ts`);
   - a shrink that misses a part writes no line (it already exits 1 and names the part);
   - the two files are tracked: the week's triage package (brief section 0, G138's text) commits the appended lines.
3. **Docs (the same diff):** brief section 0's weekly extreme night gets one clause naming `--rate` (the line text only; the queue row is the coordinator's); `tests/golden/README.md` gets the two files' row.

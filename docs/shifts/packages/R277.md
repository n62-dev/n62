### ron, R277: the entry line on touch: a drag scrolls, a tap places the caret, a hold selects the term, branch `p1/ron-linetouch2`
**Lane:** A, Opus, no device (A139 checks it on the Tab One). **Starts when:** now (R276 merged; run 1047 stopped at step 5 on `p1/ron-linetouch`, left as it is); in v0.1.0 (owner, 2026-11-12).
**Why:** (owner, 2026-11-10, points 2 and 5, rule agreed) on a long line the only way to move sideways is to drag a selection; the G2 scrolls. MathLive selects on every touch drag and scrolls only to follow the caret; our sole hook is `keepTouchCaret` (`entry/field.ts:161-187`, R42, R47). Selecting by dragging is "annoying and not really nice to use".
**Read:** (line numbers as of c583492b; recheck at start) `app/src/entry/field.ts`; `app/src/entry/controller.ts`; `app/src/entry/stops.ts`, `boxes.ts` (what a term or box is, docs/04 5.5); `App.svelte:625-640` (`touch-action: pan-x`, R53's vertical swipe that folds the keypad); MathLive's public API for its version (`app/package.json`; its own docs, downloads into scratch only) for a caret from a point and the field's scroll; the MathLive issue we filed on the touch caret (backlog.md:32, #3123).
1. **Touch only** (`pointerType` touch or pen; a mouse keeps MathLive's own drag selection). A capture listener on the field's host sees the pointer before MathLive's handlers in its shadow root.
2. **Drag sideways** past a slop of about 8 px: the line scrolls with the finger, no selection, the caret stays where it was. A vertical swipe stays R53's.
3. **Tap:** the caret at the touch point, settled to the next stop as now (R47).
4. **Hold (500 ms) on the line:** selects the term under the finger (a number with its ᴇ part, a chip, a fraction, a root, a bracket group: the box model of `boxes.ts`, the same terms R17 and L8/L9 select); the keys then act on it as on any selection (⌫ deletes it, x² raises it, R17). A hold on empty space selects nothing.
5. **The route (Opus pass 155, decisions.md "R277's line scroll"):** run 1047 found (MathLive 0.111.1 and 0.111.4, `mathlive.mjs` lines of 0.111.1): a caret from a point is public, `mf.getOffsetFromPoint(x, y, {bias})` (`types/mathfield-element.d.ts:976`), then `mf.position` or `mf.selection`; a capture `pointerdown` listener on the host that calls `preventDefault()` makes MathLive skip its own pointer handling (`:39636`, `!evt.defaultPrevented`); there is no public sideways scroll (the scroller is the shadow `[part=content]`, `:39265`; MathLive sets its `scrollLeft` only to follow the caret, `:39836`, on a drag past the edge and on a wheel; the commands are scrollIntoView, scrollToStart, scrollToEnd). So: the drag sets `scrollLeft` on `mf.shadowRoot` `[part=content]`, in one small function in `entry/field.ts` (our only write into MathLive's shadow DOM; `field.ts` and `namemarks.ts` already read it); a missing part makes the drag a no-op (no selection, no throw), and a vitest or e2e test fails when the part is missing or its `scrollLeft` does not move the drawn line, so a MathLive update shows here first. Tap and hold use the public calls only. No MathLive patch.
   **M6** (MathLive issue; the owner files it, nothing goes out from a worker): "A mathfield wider than its content area can only be scrolled sideways by a wheel or by dragging a selection past its edge. On touch, a sideways drag always selects, so a long formula cannot be panned without changing the selection. Please add a public horizontal scroll (e.g. a `scrollLeft` property or a `scrollBy(dx)` command on `MathfieldElement`) or a touch mode where a drag pans and a long press selects. Version 0.111.4; related #3123."
6. docs/05 (the entry line) and docs/04 5.5: the touch rule, in place.
**Done when:** Playwright touch tests (Pixel 7 project) show: a sideways drag on a line wider than the field scrolls it and leaves the selection collapsed; a tap puts the caret at the touched term; a hold selects that term; a vertical swipe still folds the keypad; the core list and the cursor and ⌫ grids pass.

### ron, R271: a result chip may stand after ±, branch `p1/ron-sigmachip`
**Lane:** A, Opus (marks: a σ with its own facts). **Starts when:** –.
**Why:** (night review 2026-11-09, fh-4, S3; owner 2026-11-09: allow a result chip after ±, with its own digits and marks) In the unit view, `10.1 ±` followed by a result chip is refused: `input/parse.ts:518` throws `parse.pmNeedsSigma` ("± needs a plain number after it: 12.3 ± 0.1", `i18n/enParser.ts:91`). So `sem(L)` has to be retyped, and it loses digits. Example, five readings 10.1, 10.3, 9.9, 10.0 and 10.2: mean 10.1, sem 0.0707106781187 (oracle). This changes R28's literal rule for a chip and nothing else. vfh-1: a σ that is not a literal today gets the wrong message, "± goes right after a number".
**Read:** line numbers as of `53d89cae2`; recheck at start. `app/src/input/parse.ts:505-535`; `app/src/input/unitFormula.ts`; how a chip carries its facts (`app/src/state/results.ts`: `chipOf`, `chipFactsOf`); `app/src/units/evaluate.ts` (where σ enters); decisions.md R28; the ± rows of the parser and units tests.
1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1.**
   - **Parser and evaluator rows:**
     - `10.1 ± <chip of sem, 0.0707106781187>` gives the full value as σ, checked against the oracle.
     - A σ chip marked ⚠ makes the result ⚠. A σ chip marked ≈ makes it ≈ at the σ's digits.
     - A chip whose unit has another dimension is refused (a σ shares the value's unit).
     - A negative or complex chip value is refused with its own message.

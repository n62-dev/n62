### gorge, G389: a typed product of SI base units is shown as its named SI unit, with two exceptions, branch `p1/gorge-namedsi`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Sonnet (one rule in `shownUnits`, pinned by tests). **Starts when:** after the v0.1.0 tag (0.2 work).
**Why:** (the owner, 2026-11-16: yes, named SI units; sofia's review, finding 13, for the limits.)
- **Today:** a result with no end slot is shown in the typed units merged into one product (G25, `app/src/units/build.ts:458-460` `shownUnits`). So:
  - `5 kg · 2 m/s²` shows `10 kg·m/s²`
  - `2 kg · 3 m · 4 m/s³` shows `24 kg·m²/s³`
  - `2 A · 3 s` shows `6 A·s` (`tools/probes/unit-result.ts`)
- **What an engineer expects** is N, W and C. N is offered today only as the first entry in the row of other units.
- **The exceptions:**
  - **G25's own reason** applies to `kg·m²/s²`: it is a torque as often as an energy, and J would hide the torque.
  - **A rate:** `1/s` is a decay constant as often as a frequency, and Hz would claim the frequency (docs/04 4.3, "Hz never Bq").
  - So neither is named.

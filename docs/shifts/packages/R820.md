### ron, R820: while a sheet takes letters, the key faces and the pop show the letter the key types, branch `p1/ron-sheetfaces`
**Lane:** the main queue, before the tag (owner, 2026-11-20), Sonnet. **Starts when:** after R729, which rewrites `face()` and `showPop` too (two packages on one function, one after the other). No lock change: faces are drawn, not pinned (`spec/layout-lock.yaml:2-20`); no key's action changes.

**Why** (sandra's probe at 412 × 815, N62 Dark, and a vitest probe on phase-1 7efa1e96):
- In Vars, Find, Memory and Store a tap on a letter key types its letter (`views/sheetKeys.ts:50-58` sheetKey, `:79-92` findKey, `:105-114` storeKey, routed at `keyboard/route.ts:306-312`), but the face still says Vars, sin, Tmpl: `face()` reads only Shift, Alpha and CAS (`keyboard/Keypad.svelte:66-67`). The pop shows `face()` (`:167`), so it shows `sin` while the tap types `g`. Only the flick-down bubble shows a letter (`:197`).
- **The bubble's case is wrong in Find:** it shows `letterOf(k)` (`A`) while findKey types `a` (`sheetKeys.ts:85`).
- **The dimmed keys in Find are wrong under Shift:** `dead()` asks `sheetKey` (`Keypad.svelte:83`), routing asks `findKey`, and neither `dead()` nor the face sees Shift. Under Shift in Find, Vars, Math, Tmpl, x, sin … (findKey `none`) are drawn lit, and − . space + (findKey types `-` `.` ` ` `+`) are drawn dimmed. Without Shift the two agree.
- **The case each sheet types** (the probe): Vars, Find and Memory (one sheet, id `find`) type lower case (docs/04 3.7; their filter matches either case, `views/sheet.svelte.ts:23-30`); Store types `A` (Home's names are A–Z, and `A` and `a` are different names); the other filtered lists type `A` (`letterOf`, `sheet.test.ts:260`). The faces show the case typed, whatever it is.
- docs/05 3.6 (:124) says "The faces stay as they are"; docs/05 3.7 (:139) says the pop "shows what the tap types".

**The result:**
- While a sheet is open, a key whose tap there types a character (`letter`) shows that character as its face and its pop, in the case it types; a `pick` digit, ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶, Enter, Esc, ⌫, Shift, Alpha and the `pass` and `line` keys keep their face; a `none` key keeps its face, dimmed. Under Shift or Alpha the same, for what the key types in that layer.
- The flick bubble in a sheet shows what the flick types there (Find: `a`, Store in Home: `A`), ✕ for nothing.
- `dead()` in a sheet is `none` from the same function as the face, so the dimmed keys are the keys that do nothing, Store included (a plain digit before a letter: dimmed; after one: lit).
- While the face shows the key's letter, its letter line is empty (the letter is not drawn twice); the legends are fitted again when a sheet opens or closes and when its query empties or fills.
- The letter on a face in a sheet is upright: it goes into a query, not onto the line as a variable (R142 does not apply).
- Shut sheets, the faces and pops are unchanged.

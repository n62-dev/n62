### ron, R743: a swipe from the right deletes behind a red strip that grows with it; a swipe to the right does nothing, branch `p1/ron-swipered`
**Lane:** the freeze's fix lane, Sonnet (owner, 2026-11-17). **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-17 (the red bar, delete from the right), before the tag; the swipe from the left does nothing, sandra's recommendation, which the owner kept (2026-11-17). **Starts when:** after R727 in the fix lane (owner, 2026-11-17); R741 and R742 (zebra rows over this strip) follow after the tag.

**Why:**
- Today a swipe either way deletes (`views/rowSwipe.ts:1-8`, `swipeOut` `:24-29`), and the row only fades (`place`, `:84-88`): nothing says "this will delete" before the lift.
- One direction leaves the other free and gives a wrong-way swipe a safe result. A swipe to the right starts near the left edge, where Android's back gesture lives (R700: Back drops a pick or unfolds), so a row action there would compete with Back.
- Copy is already the hold (R276) and Store ▶ is in the bar; pick is the tap. A second gesture for any of them adds a way to do by accident what another way already does.

**The result:**
- **Finger moving left** (offset < 0): the row follows; the strip it uncovers at the row's right is `--error`, as wide as the offset, with "Delete" and a bin glyph in `--on-error` at 75 % strength. Past 40 % of the row's width (`SWIPE_SHARE`) the label goes to full strength and bold (armed), and one 10 ms pulse runs when Key vibration is on (the key pulse's helper). The lift past 40 %, or a fling left, deletes as now (R98's note, Undo). Back under 40 %, the label dims again and the lift springs back.
- **Finger moving right** (offset > 0): a rubber band, `24 * (1 - exp(-x / 48))` px at most, no colour, springs back on the lift; a fling right does nothing.
- **Unchanged:** the 10 px decision, vertical wins (`DECIDE_PX`), a long `.in` or `.out` scrolls first in both directions (`share`, R333), a running row does not slide, the drag's click is swallowed.
- The row no longer fades (`opacity` goes): the strip shows the progress.
- TalkBack cannot swipe: ☰ Delete (R720's row 3) stays its way; the strip is `aria-hidden`.

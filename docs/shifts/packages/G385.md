### gorge, G385: a temperature difference in brackets takes part in arithmetic, branch `p1/gorge-tempdiffarith`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Opus (units). **Starts when:** –. A feature before the freeze of Tue 11-17 05:00 (the owner, 2026-11-16: "allow in arithmetic", over Opus pass 203's G370 shape).
**Why:** G370 (merged with G370f1, f5b9918c) makes `T1 − T2` of one absolute scale a difference only when it is the whole formula; decisions.md "G370's shape" keeps `(80 °C − 20 °C) + 5 K`, a product and a power refused. An engineer's heat formula, `m·c·(T1 − T2)`, is the common case and is refused today. The owner (2026-11-16): a bracketed difference takes part in a longer formula.
**The rule:** a bracketed group that is exactly `(T1 − T2)`, two temperatures of one absolute scale (°C − °C, °F − °F), anywhere in a formula (also nested in more brackets), is a temperature interval in K, as G370's whole-formula difference is, with the same check that neither side is below 0 K and the same ± handling. The rest of the formula then follows today's rules with that interval in place: + − with K or another such group, × ÷ by anything, a power. Everything else keeps today's refusal and message: an unbracketed `T1 − T2` inside a longer formula (`2*80 °C − 20 °C` is not a difference), two scales in one group (the G370 message naming them), an absolute temperature anywhere outside such a group (`(80 °C − 20 °C) + 5 °C` stays refused, G17), a sum of two temperatures.

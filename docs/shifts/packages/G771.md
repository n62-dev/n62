### gorge, G771: an inner integral is checked for convergence, so no divergent double integral shows "=" with a number, branch `p1/gorge-innerconverge`
**Lane:** the freeze's fix lane, Opus. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-19 ("make the workpackages"), before the tag: wrong answers. **Starts when:** after G770. n62-next's G513, G613 and G621 rebase on it.

**Why (F471, wrong answers with "="):** sandra's probe and sofia's check, 2026-11-19 (phase-1 646542e8, scratchpad `owner-bugs/D`, `verify`):
- `∫₁²∫₋₁¹ x/y² dy dx` shows **−3** with "=" in Home and CAS. True: +∞ (x/y² ≥ 0 and not integrable at y = 0 for every x; Tonelli). Home must say "The integral does not converge" (G78).
- `∫₋₁¹ x/y dy` shows **0** with "="; `∫₋₁¹ x/y² dy` shows **−x−x**.
- `∫₀¹∫₀¹ 1/(x−y) dy dx` shows **0** with "=": diverges (0 is only its principal value); `singular(1/(x-y),y)` gives `[x]`, which converge.ts drops.
- With ≈: `∫₀¹∫₋₁¹ |y − sin(10⁵⁰)| dy dx` shows `[2,1.35506364264]`; true 1.62358264688 (oracle). The single integral shows ⚠ correctly; the nested one is not read (`homeEval.ts:682` giacPair ignores the list).
- **Cause:** `converge.ts:312` (`onlyNames(body,[v])`) skips any integrand with a name other than its own variable, so an inner integral, or one with a free parameter, is never checked; the singular-point list with a symbolic point is dropped.

**The result:**
- An inner integral (and a single integral with free names) is checked with each enclosing variable set to **two generic interior points** of its interval (e.g. a + (b−a)·0.381966 and a + (b−a)·0.723607), never the midpoint or simple fractions (sofia: `∫₀¹∫₀¹ 1/(|x−½|+y) dy dx` converges, oracle 1.90954250488, though its inner integral diverges at x = ½). A divergence at either point refuses with G78's words; an error or interruption there means "unchecked", shown with ⚠, never "=".
- A singular point that depends on an enclosing variable (`[x]`) is checked at those points too.
- A nested numeric result that comes back as a list reads as unconfirmed (⚠), like the single integral.
- A single integral with a free name that diverges for every value of it (`∫₋₁¹ x/y² dy`) is refused; one that converges keeps Giac's algebra in CAS as today (G513's rule).

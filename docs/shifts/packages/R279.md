### ron, R279: Select grows the selection, Deselect shrinks it, by the box model, branch `p1/ron-selectgrow`
**Lane:** A, Opus, no device. **Starts when:** after the v0.1.0 tag and R277 (it reuses R277's term).
**Why:** (owner, 2026-11-10) a selection by keys, as Undo and Redo are edits by keys: modern editors' "expand selection", not on the G2. Every key already acts on a selection (R17; `spec/keyboard.yaml:11-30`, `app/test/selection.spec.ts`), but the only way to make one is a finger. R278 puts the two actions on r2c4 and r2c5.
**Read:** (line numbers as of 89732bdd; recheck at start) `app/src/entry/boxes.ts`, `stops.ts`, `field.ts` (`selectENumber`, `selectChip`, `selectMatrix`, `selectCompound`: the terms R17, L8 and L9 select); R277's term-under-the-finger function; `app/src/keyboard/route.ts` (one owner per key, docs/04 5.5); docs/04 5.5.
1. Two actions, `special: selectGrow` and `special: selectShrink`, routed to the entry line only (a sheet, Find, a slot or a picked row: unchanged, the key does nothing there).
2. **Grow:** nothing selected: the term before the cursor (the same term the power key would take: a number with its ᴇ part, a chip, a fraction, a root, a bracket group, a function call with its argument); each further press: the next enclosing step (the operand of the operator around it, then the enclosing box or bracket, then the whole line). At the whole line it stays.
3. **Shrink:** back one step along the same chain (the chain is kept while the selection is unchanged); from the first step it drops the selection and leaves the cursor where it was before the first Grow. Any other key, ◀, ▶ or a tap drops the chain (◀ and ▶ put the cursor at that edge of the selection).
4. The chain is plain TypeScript on the box model (a pure function from the line and a range to the next range), so it is unit-tested without MathLive.
5. Unit tests per step on `2+3·4`, `\frac{1+2}{3}`, `\sqrt{x^2+1}`, `sin(2x)+1`, a chip, `1.5ᴇ-3`; e2e: Grow twice, x², the line is `2+(3·4)²`; Grow, Shrink, Shrink leaves no selection.
**Done when:** the tests pass; with nothing selected every other key is unchanged (`selection.spec.ts` passes); the cursor and ⌫ grids pass.

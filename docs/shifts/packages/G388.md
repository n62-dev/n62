### gorge, G388: an equation in the unit view checks its units, then its values, branch `p1/gorge-unitequation`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Sonnet (a verdict in place of a wrong-reason refusal, pinned by tests). **Starts when:** G387 merged; after the v0.1.0 tag (0.2 work).
**Why:** (sandra's plan, revised; sofia's review, findings 11, 12 and 18.)
- **The wrong reason:** `10=5\cdot2` reaches the unit view: `slotFormula` gives u1–u3 and the end slot, and the parser gives `10*pasu1=(5*pasu2)*(2*pasu3)`. With N, kg and m/s² the evaluator refuses it with "A function takes a pure number (sin, cos and tan also an angle)", and it does the same when the units do not match (`tools/probes/unit-result.ts '10*{N}=5*{kg}*2*{m / s^2}'`; sofia's review, finding 18).
- **The other relations:** `x*{m}<2*{m}` gives "The unit view could not evaluate this".
- **Why it matters:** checking that both sides of an equation have one unit is the most common check of a formula. A verdict on units alone must not read as a pass when the values differ (principle 3).

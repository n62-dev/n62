**Why:** (sandra S1 probe `../drafts/2026-11-22-owner-bugs3/s1-probes/zz-sandra-s1.spec.ts`, rerun by sofia; fake engine, phase-1)
- **Hidden after Clear for good.** `app/src/views/History.svelte:204` draws the mark only when `items.length === 0 && !undo`. The Undo copy of Clear or Delete lives until that view's next Enter (`app/src/state/session.svelte.ts:468-471, 686-689`), which adds a row, and it is stored, so a relaunch keeps it. - **Cropped on a short history.** `.empty` sits with `margin: auto` in the scrolling column (`History.svelte:289-312`, `.faint` 128 px high). At 360 × 640 with the keys up the history is 88 px high and 34 % of the mark shows, its foot only (`s1-17-9-small.png`); in landscape 915 × 412 the history is 16 px and 0 % shows.
- **A flash at start.** With a stored history the empty state is in the page for about 10 ms before the rows arrive: it renders before `restoreHistory` sets `historyRead` (`session.svelte.ts:276, 793`).

**The result:**
- The mark and the hint ("Type a calculation and press Enter.") show whenever the open view has no rows and its stored history has been read. With an Undo note the note stays the history's last row at the foot, and the mark and hint sit centred in the space above it.
- The mark's height is the space left, up to `--mark-h` (128 px), never cropped. Under 40 px it is left out and the hint stays. The hint is never cut.
- Its tap (the credits sheet, R157) and its colour are unchanged (R874 changes the colour).

**Lane:** by day, Sonnet, on the freeze day (Tue 11-17) after the last merge that changes a stored shape. **Starts when:** the freeze.
**Why:** v0.1.0 is the oldest reader for good, and what it wrote must restore in every later build.
- The rule is "a changed stored shape raises `DATA_VERSION`" (`app/src/state/storage.ts:26-32`). The practice was to add a field without it: `mark` on a definition (`app/src/engine/defs.ts:39`) and `keys` in the press counts (`app/src/state/backup.ts:232`) came at version 4 with no step. v0.1.0's reader is lenient exactly there: `validDefs` drops a field it does not know, the settings check passes an unknown top-level field, a row's `fmt` is read back unchecked (`app/src/state/storedItem.ts:48-50`). So a newer backup with a new field and the old stamp restores into v0.1.0 with the field silently gone; with a raised stamp it is refused in words (`backup.ts:294`).
- The fixture that proves "an old backup restores" (`app/src/state/fixtures/backup-2026-11.json`) holds six of the thirteen parts and two Home rows: no unit row, no CAS row, no preset. The update test starts from a dev build (`tests/fixtures/update-from-ef857a6.json`).

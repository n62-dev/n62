### ron, R741: a tap anywhere on a history row picks it; a tap on the picked row's empty part drops the pick, branch `p1/ron-rowtap`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, after the tag (owner, 2026-11-17), night-safe, Sonnet. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-17 ("a tap on a row's empty area should pick the row"). **Starts when:** R743 merged (both edit `views/History.svelte`); after R376 in v0.1.1's order. Before R742.

**Why:**
- Only the input and the result are buttons (`views/History.svelte:213`, `:233-241`). The rest of the row's box takes the tap in the article's `onclick` (`:210`), which runs `toggleSigma` (`:102-106`): it opens a σ line on a σ row and does nothing on every other row. The history's own handler (`:94-96`) sees a tap inside `.item` and does nothing either.
- Measured at 412 × 815 (phase-1 228bc43f, `tools/probes/history-1117/probe-folded.mjs`): on the row `2+3 = 5` the two buttons cover **7 %** of the row (1,848 of 25,135 px²). A tap to the right of `2+3` hits `ARTICLE.item` and picks nothing.
- An error row's message is a `<span>` (`:226`), so only its input picks it.

**The result:**
- A first tap anywhere on a row (input, result, mark, the space between, a uses or σ line, an error message) picks the row, as ▲ does. The pick is the row, not a half: the bar's Store ▶ and Copy › act on its result, ◀ (R720) recalls its input.
- A second tap on the picked row: on the input it recalls, on the result it inserts (R147, as now); anywhere else it drops the pick.
- A σ row (R40, σ on tap): the tap that picks also shows the σ line; the tap that drops the pick hides it.
- The reason line (R124) keeps its own tap (full text), and the Cancel button its own.
- A hold, a swipe and a drag that scrolls never pick (their clicks are swallowed: `rowSwipe.ts` `click`, `apps/longpress.ts`).
- A running row can be picked (as ▲ can); a note row cannot (`pickRows.ts`).

**Read:** `views/History.svelte:86-106,206-262`, `state/pickRows.ts`, `views/rowSwipe.ts` (`click`), `apps/longpress.ts`, `App.svelte:365-370` (`pick`), `app/test/rowgestures.spec.ts`, `rows.spec.ts`, `fold.spec.ts:197-245`, `History.test.ts`.

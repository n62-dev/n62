**Why:**
- Find is the tap of the key in the G2's Math place (`spec/keyboard.yaml:136`, `id: math`; R4a, R802). The unit view keeps it there (`spec/unit-pad.yaml:53`, `units/pad.ts:69-70`), because the pad never moves a key (`unit-pad.yaml:10-11`).
- Its look is that of Vars and Tmpl: `--key-fn`, the text colour, 17 px (sandra's probe, `../drafts/2026-11-22-owner-bugs3/s2-probe-legends.spec.ts`).
- Way A keeps Enter the only key filled with the accent, and uses no ring, which R729 makes mean "armed".
- `--ring` (the entry field's outline) against `--key-fn` measures at least 3.74:1 in every family and brightness, normal and high contrast (`s2-contrast.mjs` on `s2-tokens.log`): N62 Light 3.74, N62 Dark 3.91, Solarized Dark 7.86. Above the 3:1 for telling controls apart.

**The look (the mock-up's column A):**
- **A lens** (a circle and a handle, about 12-13 px at 360 px wide) in the key's free top-right corner, in `--ring`.
- **A 4 px bar** along the key's foot, inside the key (an inset shadow above the R156 key shadow), in `--ring`.
- **The face** keeps "Find▾" at its full fitted size, and the legends (Mem▾, B) keep their places.
- **Plain keys:** lens and bar in `--key-text`.
- **High contrast:** `--ring` as given.
- **Drawn dead** (Plot, R777): both in `--key-text-dead`.
- **The same in the unit view** (same key id), and in the wide layout.
- **CSS only:** a `::before` on `.key[data-key="math"]` drawn with gradients. No CSS mask: `padarrows.spec.ts:156-158` forbids one anywhere in the keypad, because Chromium reads the bare mask only from 120 while the WebView floor is 111, and a masked layer moved faces 1.2 px. No markup and no SVG in the template.

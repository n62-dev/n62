### ron, R877: setting rows, part 2: switches drawn on and off, ticks of a multi-pick as boxes, read-only values muted, branch `p1/ron-switchrows`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, Opus. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-22 (S6 point 7, hunt A: "the row patterns of hunt A"; `docs/mockups/settings-rows.html` section 2, from R876). **Starts when:** after R876.

**Why** (sandra's walk `s6-probe-walk.spec.ts`, `../drafts/2026-11-22-owner-bugs3/s6-settings.json`, outside the repo):
- A switch that is off draws nothing at its end (`app/src/views/Sheet.svelte:259`: `.tick` holds "✓" or ""), so High contrast and Plain keys read as headings or actions; the same for Key vibration, Key click and Word › Signed.
- One ✓ means three things: a switch that is on (Appearance), one of several picks that are on (Numbers › Fmt key cycle: five ticks, any number on, `views/modeSheet.ts` `fmtTicks`), and the current one of a list (the theme list, Digits' list, a key's slot choices).
- Read-only values look like settings: Counters' numbers and dates are in `--shift` (`.value`, `Sheet.svelte:586-590`), the colour of a value you change; Data's "Kept, but the browser may delete it…" (`views/dataSection.svelte.ts:75`) is drawn like the action rows above it.

**The result:** **switch row**: a drawn switch at the row's end in both states (track and knob, on in `--key-on`); the whole row toggles, as today. **Box row** (several may be on): an empty or ticked box. **Pick in a list** (one is current): ✓ as today. **Info row** (a value only, no `run`, `adjust`, `submenu` or `options`): value in `--muted`, label in `--text`; a tap does nothing (as today: `keep` with no run). Screen readers: a switch or box row is `role="menuitemcheckbox"` with `aria-checked`, a pick in a list `menuitemradio`.

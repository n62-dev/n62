**Why:** (sandra S1, confirmed by sofia) `android/app/src/main/res/values/styles.xml:16-22` pins `statusBarColor`, `navigationBarColor` and `windowBackground` to `@color/n62_dark_bg` #092726 with light icons (A59, decisions.md:43, made when the bars were white in the system's light mode). Nothing changes them later: `app/src/theme/lookApply.ts:53-55` sets only `<meta name="theme-color">`, which the PWA's browser reads and Capacitor's WebView ignores. targetSdk 36 (`android/variables.gradle:4`): from Android 15 the bars are drawn edge to edge and their colours ignored, so what shows behind them is the window background, the same #092726. Solarized Light thus has a dark teal strip above and below a pale yellow screen.

- The splash and the first frame before the page's JS stay N62 Dark (A59's part that stays; `app/scripts/android-bars.test.ts` keeps them equal to style.css).
- The PWA is unchanged (its theme-color already follows).
- No layout change: the page does not move under the bars.

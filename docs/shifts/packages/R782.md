### ron, R782: the bar's three workspace slots: Home fixed, slot 2 CAS, slot 3 "+", a second tap reassigns, branch `p1/ron-barslots`
**Lane:** the freeze's fix lane, Opus. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-20 (the owner's "Option B" bar; replaces R771's tabs and R722's tab row). **Starts when:** after R771. Lock: `spec/layout-lock.yaml` `bar:` changes (decided here: the owner approved it); state the new order in the report.

**Why:** today's tabs change width per app and the Apps ▾ tile is a fourth way to switch (`StatusLine.svelte`, docs/05 3.2). The owner wants one scalable scheme for many apps: three fixed-width slots that never shift.

**The result:**
- **Left anchor, three slots** of one fixed width each (about 50 dp at 360; short permanent names: "Home", "CAS", the app's short name of at most 4 letters, added to the registry per app), grouped in a recessed track (token `--bar-track`) with a hairline (`--bar-hair`) between the slots and the middle bay and an 8 px gap. Colours are tokens per theme, never written in a `.svelte` file (R12b).
- **Slot 1 is Home:** fixed, not removable, never shows the ◢. **Slot 2 defaults to CAS** and is reassignable. **Slot 3 defaults to "+"** and is reassignable. A slot holds one app id or "+"; an app is in at most one slot (picking one already in a slot switches to that slot).
- **A tap on a slot** switches to it. **A tap on "+"** opens the app picker at once (the Apps sheet). **A second tap on the already-active slot 2 or 3** opens the picker to reassign it, with a "Clear" row back to "+". No long press anywhere. The small corner triangle ◢ shows only on the active slot 2 or 3, so the affordance matches the tap.
- The Apps ▾ tile goes (the picker is the "+" or the second tap). The slots persist in the settings and survive a reload and a sync; the first run has Home, CAS, "+". An app's own state stays with the app, not the slot (clearing a slot loses nothing).

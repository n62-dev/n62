- **The heap may grow to 1 GB** and never shrinks until the worker restarts: `-sALLOW_MEMORY_GROWTH=1 -sINITIAL_MEMORY=64MB -sMAXIMUM_MEMORY=1GB` (`engine/giac/build-wasm.sh:267`).
- **The cap was checked once, by hand, on the tablet** (`tests/core/ONE-TIME.md:8`: CAS `makemat(1,30000,30000)` gives "Out of memory" and the app stays up, G24); the automated test runs in Node (`tests/engine/memcap-test.mjs`). The Fairphone and a small phone were never tried.
- **On a phone with little RAM, Android may kill the WebView's renderer before the wasm cap is reached.** The app has no handler: no `onRenderProcessGone` under `android/app/src/main` (grep; `MainActivity.java` is the only activity). What Capacitor 8.5.3 does by default is unverified. Then G24's "Out of memory" path (`app/src/engine/dead.ts:19-24`) never runs and the app may close with the half-typed line.
- **A known input reaches 525 MB:** F187, Area's `romberg((e_/x^x),x,exact(0),exact(1))` at Digits 30 (`docs/backlog.md:79`).

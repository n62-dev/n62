### ron, R576: Shift + Enter (≈) after a typed line gives the line's decimal, not `1/3·approx(□)`, branch `p1/ron-approxline`
**Lane:** A, night-safe, Sonnet (one key's action; e2e key sequences). **Starts when:** the owner's yes to the behaviour below (one line in T4's SUMMARY.md, "Needs the owner").

**Why** (F353, found by T4 on 2026-11-16, not the owner; sessions S07 "a label that lies" and S16 "exact against decimal (Shift Enter)"): the Enter key's Shift legend is ≈, and the natural order is type the sum, then ≈. Today Shift + Enter only inserts `approx(□)` at the cursor (`spec/keyboard.yaml:260-263`, `shift: { insert: "\\operatorname{approx}\\left(#0\\right)" }`), so after a typed line it lands **behind** the line. Playwright, Pixel 7, real engine, phase-1 e93dec77:

| Keys (CAS) | Line after Shift + Enter | Enter gives |
|---|---|---|
| `1 ÷ 3 ▶` Shift + Enter | `1/3·approx(□)` | unsupported command \placeholder (F351) |
| Shift + Enter `1 ÷ 3` | `approx(1/3)` | ≈ 0.333333333333 |
| `√2` Enter, then Shift + Enter on the empty line | `approx(□)` | (nothing to approximate) |

So ≈ works only as a prefix typed before the expression; the key never does what its legend says when pressed last, and the product it builds is a trap (an implicit × the user never typed).

**Behaviour (T4's recommendation, the owner decides):**
- a line with text and no selection: Shift + Enter wraps the **whole line** in `approx(…)` and evaluates it at once (one press, like Enter);
- a selection: wraps the selection, as today (`#0` takes it), no evaluation;
- an empty line: wraps Ans (`approx(Ans)`) and evaluates, so √2 Enter, Shift + Enter gives ≈ 1.41421356237.

**Read:** `spec/keyboard.yaml:260-263`; the key action runner (`app/src/entry/controller.ts` `run`, `app/src/entry/field.ts`: how `insert` fills `#0` from the selection); `app/test/bksp.spec.ts:269` (the template's ⌫ case); `app/test/cursor.spec.ts:21` (the cursor in approx); docs/06's row of the Enter key.

   - `1 ÷ 3 ▶` Shift + Enter → one new row ≈ 0.333333333333, the line empty; CAS the same.
   - `√2` Enter, Shift + Enter → a row ≈ 1.41421356237.
   - **Controls** (pass before and after): Shift + Enter then `1 ÷ 3` Enter → ≈ 0.333333333333 (the prefix order keeps working); a selection (Select twice on `2+3`, Shift + Enter) gives `approx(2+3)` on the line, not evaluated; bksp.spec.ts:269 and cursor.spec.ts:21; the core list.
2. **The change:** the Enter key's shift action becomes a `special` (e.g. `approxLine`) that applies the three cases above; the insert stays for the selection case. The key's legend stays ≈.

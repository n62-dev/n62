### ron, R726: Shift locks on a second tap as Alpha does, a tap on the bar's ⇧/α sign ends any Shift or Alpha, and the modifier rules join the layout lock, branch `p1/ron-modlocks`
**Lane:** the freeze's fix lane, Sonnet (owner, 2026-11-17). **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-17 (one lock rule; the drafts folder's 2026-11-17-keys/ANSWER.md, point 1, outside the repo); before the tag, because it changes what a second Shift tap does. **Starts when:** after R722 (the ⇧/α slot sits on the bar) and R724 (the folded bar shows the slot); in the fix lane after R725. Must merge by Mon 11-23.

**Why:**
- **Two rules for one job.** A second Shift tap turns Shift off (`session.svelte.ts:363-370`); a second Alpha tap locks Alpha (`:396-399`).
- **Shift locks only by a flick up** (`:372-377`, `route.ts:501-511`). TalkBack, a mouse and a hardware keyboard cannot flick, so they have no Shift lock.
- **The bar shows a lock it cannot end.** The ⇧/α slot is a span (`StatusLine.svelte:95-101`). Folded (R724) there is no Shift or Alpha key on screen, but a lock from before the fold stays.
- The G2 record does not say what its second Shift or Alpha press does (`keys.yaml:65, 72`, "not checked"). **To verify before the build:** annabell or the owner presses Shift, Shift and Alpha, Alpha on the Virtual Calculator and notes it in `tests/golden/g2.yaml`.

**The result:**
- **Shift:** tap 1 arms it for one key, tap 2 locks it, tap 3 ends it (Alpha's rule, keyboard.yaml:68).
  - While Alpha is armed or locked, a Shift tap still flips the case of one letter, as now (`:402-405`). A second Shift tap there does not lock.
  - The flick up on Shift still locks and unlocks. One lock at a time, as now.
- **The bar's ⇧/α sign** is a button while Shift or Alpha is on. A tap ends any of them, armed or locked, and the sign empties.
  - It follows R722's bar rule: it acts only on a pointer that went down on it.
  - An empty slot is not a button: a tap there does nothing and is not counted.
  - TalkBack: `status.modOff`, "{mod} on. Double-tap to turn it off".
- **Counts:** a Shift or Alpha tap counts as now (`s.taps.tap`); the sign's tap counts nothing.

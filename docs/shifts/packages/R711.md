### ron, R711: a setting for where menus sit: over the keys, or above them with the keypad free, branch `p1/ron-menuplace`
**Lane:** A, Opus (a second placement of the one sheet; the owner's eye on the phone and the tablet). **Starts when:** after the v0.1.0 tag; R700 merged (Back), R704 merged (deep levels). **The owner's answer (2026-11-16, T5):** yes, as a setting. The phone's default stays "over the keys"; the wide layout's default is "above the keys"; with a removal test.
- **What it changes:**
  - **R144, in "above the keys" only:** a tap on a digit key picks a row instead of closing the sheet. Every other key keeps R144's close.
  - **R21's placement, as an option:** the sheet rises over the keypad's maths rows today.
  - **Nothing in the layout lock moves:** the same rows keep the same digits.

**Why:**
- **The digit keys are hidden on a phone (T5 map):** a pick sheet covers rows 3–9 (R21), so the digit keys and Enter are under it. A row is picked only by a tap on it. "☰, 3" works only on a hardware keyboard, though every list is numbered (docs/05 3.1) for that.
- **A row moves, a key does not:** a row's height on the screen differs per sheet (☰ has 10 rows, Mode 6, Apps 4); the 3 key never moves. Picking by key makes "☰, 3" a blind motion, which is docs/03 rule 8 applied to the menus.
- **The tablet wastes room:** in the wide layout the ☰ menu covers the keypad's maths rows while the stage beside it is empty (`tests/ui-walk/probe/tablet-home-menu.jpg`; docs/05 3.9).
- **The cost:**
  - On a phone the view above the keypad holds about 7 rows of 36 px at 412 × 815, so ☰'s ten rows scroll there. 1–0 still pick items 1–10 whether they are drawn or not.
  - Find, Vars and Store in already sit above the keypad (they take letters) and stay as they are.

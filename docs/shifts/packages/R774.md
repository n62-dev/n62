**Why:** sandra's probes, confirmed by sofia (scratchpad `owner-bugs/B`, `C`, `verify`):
- **Blue tap boxes:** the bar's controls, the tabs, ☰ and the sheets' buttons keep Android's default `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: rgba(51,181,229,0.4)`: a rectangular blue box on rounded chips, stacked on the tabs' own `[data-down]` fill (`StatusLine.svelte:225-228`). - **D-pad press:** a pressed arrow only goes from 0.7 to full opacity; the circle never changes (`Keypad.svelte:604-616`).

- **"Copied" moves the rows:** the notice is in the page flow (`App.svelte:524`, `.notice` at `:710`, `position: relative`, 32 px), so the rows move up 36 px and back.

**The result:**
- `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent` on `:root`; every bar chip and sheet button shows its own press fill (`[data-down]`, `--key-nav`, `border-radius: inherit`), rounded like the control.
- A pressed d-pad zone shows a 40 px round dot behind its arrow in `color-mix(in srgb, var(--key-text) 32%, transparent)`, inside the circle.
- The locked ring draws inside the key (`outline-offset: calc(-1 * var(--stroke-thick))`).
- Notices ("Copied" and the like) draw over the foot of the history (absolute, as `.rowslot.over`), so no row moves.

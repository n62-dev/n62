**Why:** (arch review 2026-11-10, HUNT.md H5, S3; confirmed at `5a583f31`)
- **The natural key order fails.** Shift + 9 (→) then Store ▶ (the exponent key's shift) gives `x → a·x² ▶ f`. Today:

- **The cause:** `relation()` (`app/src/input/parse.ts:110-133`) parses the arrow's body with `this.relation()` (`:120`), which takes the trailing `▶ f` (`:123-128`) into the body. A store is the lowest level: `value ▶ name` should bind looser than `→`, as `name := value` already does from the left.
- No wrong value (an error), but the G2-style order of keys for "define f" is the one an engineer tries first.

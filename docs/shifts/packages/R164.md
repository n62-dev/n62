**Read:** R161's diff (`git log --merges --grep R161`); the four rules above and their components' pointer or click handlers; `app/test/` specs for Settings value rows (grep `adjust`) and the status line.
1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1:** an e2e with touch pointers: each of these elements gets `data-down` on pointerdown before the next frame and loses it at the later of the lift and 80 ms; 10 taps 60 ms apart on a Settings ◀ ▶ step light it each time and step 10 times; a pointercancel clears it.

### ron, R747: a constant from Find works in Solve and Function, branch `p1/ron-appconst`
**Lane:** the freeze's fix lane, Sonnet. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-18 ("commit and push"), before the tag. **Starts when:** after R746 (both change how results and constants go on a line). No lock line changes.

**Why:** sandra's sweep, 2026-11-18 (after the owner's fraction bug): Solve › Symb E1 `x = c` with `c` picked from Find shows "constant c: its value is missing" and cannot be solved; Function F1 `x×c` the same. Find opens in the apps and puts a `\const` chip on the line (`find/finder.ts:161-163` putOnLine → `controller.ts:233` insertConst), and a user key holding a constant takes the same path (`controller.ts:248`). But the apps parse without the line's value table: `apps/solve/app.svelte.ts:64` and `apps/function/compile.ts:38` call `parseLatex` without `chips`, so `parse.ts` throws. `route.ts:554` refuses Ans and ± in an app, but not constants.

**The result (the decided way: no new storage before the tag):**
- In an app's entry line (Solve, Function, every app line whose text is stored without chips), a constant from Find or a user key goes on the line as its **value** in plain digits, editable, as one term (bracketed if it has an exponent: `\left(6.6743\times10^{-11}\right)`), not as a `\const` chip. A one-line note says so once: "In an app a constant goes in as its value" (i18n).
- Home and CAS keep the chip with its uncertainty, as today.
- The apps' lines never hold a chip they cannot value: a `\const`, `\res` or `\ans` that reaches an app line by any path is refused at insert, as `route.ts:554` refuses Ans.

### gorge, G613: an integral Giac leaves after a 1/x substitution keeps its factor, never a false integrate(…), branch `p1/gorge-mobiusrest`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Opus (a Giac patch; a false CAS answer with no mark, and a wrong number after it). **Starts when:** –. Before the tag (not the freeze: it needs an integrand with no closed form).
**Why:** (T6b bug hunt 2026-11-16, F428; probes `../drafts/2026-11-16-ultra/t6b/probes/cas.mts`, `cmp.mts`, engine `_out/gsl`, phase-1 `418f125e`.)
- **What happens:** CAS `int(tan(1/x),x,1,2)` shows `integrate(-tan(1/x)/((1/x)^2),x,1,2)`, unmarked. That integral is ∫₁² −x²·tan(1/x) dx = −1.79893515090, not ∫₁² tan(1/x) dx = 0.862320919638 (`source: oracle`, mpmath quad, 20 digits).
- **And then a wrong number:** CAS `int(tan(1/(x-1/2)),x,2,5)`, then `approx(Ans)` (the Ans chip, as the view sends it) → `-9.39554385269` ⚠ (unaudited integrate); right 1.17187230762 (mpmath quad 30 digits: 1.17187230761828945044593829646). `int(tan(1/x),x,1,2)`, then `approx(Ans)` → `-1.7989351509` ⚠.
- **More of the same shape** (each a false `integrate(…)` in CAS): `int(atan(1/x)/x,x,1,2)`, `int(1/asinh(3/x),x,1/2,5)` (right 4.80394888241), `int(1/(1+exp(1/x)),x,1,2)`, `int(tan(x/(x+1)),x,0,1)`, `int(tan((x+1)/(x+2)),x,0,1)`; the indefinite `int(tan(1/x),x)` = `integrate(-tan(1/x)/((1/x)^2),x)`, whose derivative is not tan(1/x).
- **Right today:** Home (G90: the numeric integration of the typed integrand, 1.17187230762 ⚠), CAS `approx(int(…))` (the same), and every 1/x substitution Giac finishes: `int(exp(-3/x),x,1,2)`, `int(cos(2/(x+3)),x,0,1)`, `int(sqrt(x/(x+1)),x,0,1)`, `int(atan(2/(x+1)),x,0,3)` agree with mpmath to 20 digits.
- **Cause (giac 2.1.0 with our patches, `../drafts/2026-11-11-parisse/archive/giac-n62-2026-11-15/intg.cc`; recheck in the pinned tarball):**
  - `intg.cc:3094` `df=derive(f,gen_x)` for the inner function f; f linear over linear (1/x, 2/(2x−1), x/(x+1)) takes the branch at 3105-3116.
  - There the integrand is rewritten in t = f(x) with its Jacobian, integrated, and what Giac could not integrate (`tmprem`, in t) goes back as `remains_to_integrate += complex_subst(tmprem,gen_x,f)` (line 3114): the factor `df` is missing.
  - The general u-substitution branch three lines below (3124) has it: `complex_subst(tmprem,gen_x,f)*df`. tan(1/x): tmprem = −tan(t)/t², put back without df gives exactly the printed `-tan(1/x)/((1/x)^2)`.
- **Why N62 misses it:** the CAS view shows Giac's unevaluated integral as an exact answer (a non-number with no float: no mark); Ans keeps it, so approx(Ans) integrates the false integrand.

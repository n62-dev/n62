### ron, R251: the RPN operation composer and its binding to a row, UI-free, branch `p1/ron-rpncompose`
**Lane:** A, day, Opus. **Starts when:** R250 is merged and the owner's yes on mh-D1.
**Why:** (mh-D1) a stack operation must be one ordinary Home row: LaTeX built from `\res` chips and handed to `Session.submit` (`app/src/state/session.svelte.ts:431`). Then there is one evaluation path, and Cancel, the marks and the audit come free. Exact stays exact because the chip's value is what the row reads. Operands leave the stack only when the row is **done**: an error, a Cancel or a refused `submit` (it answers `undefined` when `busy`) leaves the stack as it was.

**Arity (R250's `arityOf`; this package supplies the forms):**

| Arity | Key, layer | LaTeX, with A = level 2 and B = level 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | `add`, `sub`, `mul` primary | `A+B`, `A-B`, `A\times B` |
| 2 | `div`, `pow` primary | `\frac{A}{B}`, `A^{B}` |
| 1 | `sq` primary, `div` shift | the key's template with `#@` = B: `B^{2}`, `B^{-1}` |
| 1 | `sq` shift, `sin` `cos` `tan` `ln` `log` primary and shift, `neg` shift, `enter` shift | the key's template with `#0` = B |
| 0 | `emark`, `paren`, `d8` shift, `frac`, `pow` shift, the palettes, every other key | none: never reaches the composer |

A and B are `\res{k}{shown}` (`resChip`, as `app/src/entry/controller.ts:184` writes it). The templates are read from the key's action, never copied, and the one-line (`line:`) forms are never used here. The power takes the whole chip (parser golden `chip-res-04`), so a negative base needs no bracket.
**Read:** `app/src/state/session.svelte.ts:425-500` (`submit`, `chipFor`); `app/src/entry/controller.ts:180-195`; `spec/keyboard.yaml` lines 13-30 (template slots); `tests/golden/parser.yaml:523-527`. Line numbers as of de659c02; recheck at start.
1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1** (new file `app/src/entry/rpnCompose.test.ts`, a fake port; L as in R250):
   - `add` on `[L(2,"3"), L(1,"2")]` gives latex `\res{1}{2}+\res{2}{3}` and chips with exactly `res:1` and `res:2`.
   - `sub` gives `\res{1}{2}-\res{2}{3}`; `mul` `\res{1}{2}\times\res{2}{3}`; `div` `\frac{\res{1}{2}}{\res{2}{3}}`; `pow` `\res{1}{2}^{\res{2}{3}}`.
   - `sq` on `[L(4,"5")]` gives `\res{4}{5}^{2}`; `sin` gives `\sin\left(\res{4}{5}\right)`; `sq` shift `\sqrt{\res{4}{5}}`; `enter` shift `\operatorname{approx}\left(\res{4}{5}\right)`.
   - `mul` after `dup` on `[L(7,"x")]`: latex `\res{7}{x}\times\res{7}{x}`, one key `res:7` (one chip used twice is one input).
   - A spec walk over `spec/keyboard.yaml`: every primary and shift `insert` with `#0` or `#@` is either in the table above or on an explicit `RPN_NOT_UNARY` list, and every key of arity 1 has exactly one such slot and no `#?`; a new key must be classified.
   - Binding, with a fake port: on `done` with a chip, the two operands go and one level is pushed, its `k` the row's id. On status `error` or `cancelled`: the levels are unchanged. When `submit` answers `undefined` (busy): unchanged, answer `"busy"`. On `done` with no chip or with a `unit` or `unc` chip: the levels are unchanged, the row stands, answer `"noChip"` or `"units"`. Too few levels: no `submit` call, answer `"depth"`.
2. **Build `app/src/entry/rpnCompose.ts`:** `compose(levels, keyId, layer, action): { latex, chips } | null` and `applyOp(stack, port, …)`. The port is `{ submit, newest, chipFor, shownOf }`, typed from `Session` with no change to `submit` or `chipFor`. `shownOf` is the shown LaTeX of a row, the rule of `App.svelte:180-185` (R254 supplies it). UI-free; no key is wired.

The check that a key sequence and its algebraic line give the same text, mark and `full` is G302.

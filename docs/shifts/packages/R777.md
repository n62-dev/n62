### ron, R777: a key that does nothing gives no click and no press look, only the dead key's double pulse, branch `p1/ron-deadquiet`
**Lane:** the freeze's fix lane, Sonnet. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-19, before the tag ("while in Find, user keys and menu keys can be pressed but do nothing?"; "if you click out of a menu, why is a button pressed and a sound played, if nothing happens?"). **Starts when:** after R770 (it changes which keys do nothing on an open sheet). No lock change.

**Why:** sandra's probe, confirmed by sofia's own run (scratchpad `owner-bugs/A`, `verify`): in Find, 16 keys are drawn dead (`data-dead`: U1, U2, Help, Undo, Select, Fmt …); a tap on one still gives the key click, one 10 ms pulse and the press look, and nothing else. docs/05 3.7: "A press on one gives a short double haptic and no other effect."

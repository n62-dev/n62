**Why:**
- **R15b** (cluster rows 36 px) and **R21** (sheet rows down to 36 px) both "would change if 36 px keys are mis-tapped on the phone" (decisions.md, archived text).
- **Nothing counts a mis-tap,** so neither clause can ever fire.
- **The walk measured the targets ([checks.md](../../research/t5-ui/checks.md), "Tap targets"):**
  - every key of rows 0–2: 64 × 36 px on the phone;
  - sheet rows: 36–44 px;
  - the status chips: 20–39 × 24 px.

  All are under Material's 48 dp. Whether that costs anything is a question for counts, not for a guideline.
- **The owner's rule** (docs/03 rule 11): every feature has a removal test, and we act on it. This makes these two tests readable.

1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1** (vitest and e2e, `app/test/mistaps.spec.ts`):
   - **(a) An undone key:** a key press is followed within 1.5 s by ⌫, Undo or Esc that removes exactly what it typed. One "undone" count under that key and layer.
   - **(b) An unused sheet:** a sheet is opened and closed within 1 s with no pick (×, a tap outside, Back, Esc). One "unused" count under the control that opened it.
   - **(c) A neighbour:** a dead press on a key within 1 s after a tap on its neighbour. One "next to" count under the pair.
   - **(d) Not counted:** ⌫ after a key the user typed 3 s before; a sheet closed after a pick.
   - **(e) Settings › Counters:** shows the five highest of each kind, per 100 presses of that key.
   - **(f) The counts file and the backup:** both carry the new counts (R182's format; the report names the version step if one is needed).
2. **The change:**
   - Three counters beside the press counts (R127), fed from the press path and the sheet's close.
   - No timing affects what a key does.
3. **Docs (the same diff):**
   - decisions.md "R15b" and "R21": their "would change if" names the counter and its threshold. The threshold is proposed in the report, for the owner: undone presses on a cluster key above 3 % of its presses over four weeks.

### ron, R729: the key legends: nothing on a key moves, the next press is lit, armed is a ring and locked a fill on the keys and the bar, branch `p1/ron-keylegends`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, after the tag (owner, 2026-11-17), Sonnet. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-17 (the mockup: the drafts folder's 2026-11-17-keys/legends.html, outside the repo). **Starts when:** after R723 (the cells), R726 (the lock rule, the bar's ⇧/α button) and R728 if queued (else it does R728's two code points).

**Why:**
- **Text jumps with the state:** armed Shift writes its layer into the face and empties the legend line (`keyboard/Keypad.svelte:60-69`).
- **Armed and locked look alike** except on Shift and Alpha (`Keypad.svelte:654-675`); a lock is drawn three ways (`views/StatusLine.svelte:262`, `i18n/en.ts:98-101`).

**The result (as legends.html):**
- **Places are fixed.**
  - The face is always the tap.
  - The top-left legend is the Shift layer, in `--key-shift` (orange stays Shift's only, docs/05 3.10).
  - The bottom-left letter is the Alpha layer, in `--key-alpha`, in the case Alpha types in the view and state (Shift + Alpha: lower case in Home).
  - A user key's Alpha slot sits at the right end of its legend line (rows 0–2 have no letter line).
- **The next press is lit, the rest dims:**
  - **Shift armed:** each Shift legend gets a ring, an inset 1.5 px box-shadow in its colour. Faces and letters take `--key-text-dead`.
  - **Alpha armed:** each letter gets a ring; the faces of letter keys dim, and Shift legends dim. A key with no letter keeps its face lit, since Alpha there is its tap.
  - **Locked:** the same, with a fill in place of the ring (`--key-shift` / `--key-alpha`, text `--on-key-shift` / `--on-key-alpha`).
  - **Enter** uses `--on-enter` for both.
- **The Shift and Alpha keys, and the bar's ⇧/α button** (R722's bar, R726's button): a ring when armed, a fill when locked. The underline (`StatusLine.svelte:262`) and the key's outline (`Keypad.svelte:668-675`) go.
- **A lock flick's bubble** shows the sign filled (lock) or plain (unlock), not 🔒 or 🔓.
- **An empty layer has an empty corner:** no legend and no mark. While armed, a key with nothing lit is dead and dimmed, as now (docs/06 3b rule 1).
- **No mark for flicks;** R143's ▾ and R142's italics stay.
- **The key pop (R162) shows what the press types in this layer**, the text the face showed before.
- **Plain keys and High contrast** keep ring and fill: the shape tells armed from locked without colour.

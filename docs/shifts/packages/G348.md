### gorge, G348: a huge power under exp(i·x) or a negative base is unchecked, the G307 chain's shared cause, branch `p1/gorge-literalpow-cis`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Opus. **Starts when:** –.
**Why:** (Opus pass 175; the third case of the G307 chain, so a new id, brief 0c point 5) A value that reads its argument modulo 2π needs that argument to an absolute precision; past `MAX_EVAL_DIGITS` no level we run holds a huge power of pi or exp that well, and P and P + 20 agree on a random digit about one time in ten. G307f2 (a766ec9c) counts the huge power when the text calls sin, cos, tan, sec, csc or cot, but the same value comes from exp of an imaginary argument and from a power of a negative or imaginary base, and those are still left out. At cc5dd501 (`FACTS=1 node tools/probes/engine.ts`): Home `exp(i*pi^2207)` (also `e^(i*pi^2207)`) shows ≈ -0.4-0.9*i, reliableDigits 1, no warn; the value is 0.423070018226+0.906096992423*i (mpmath at 1400 and 1700 digits agree: cos and sin of pi^2207). `(-1)^(pi^2206)` is the same number (principal value exp(i·pi·pi^2206)) and shows the same ≈ -0.4-0.9*i. `exp(i*pi^2200)` and `(-1)^(pi^2200)` show ⚠ 0 only because their levels happened to disagree. G307f2's rule is also too wide: a periodic call anywhere counts every huge power, so `sin(1)+pi^3000` (right value, ≈ before G307f2) is now ⚠ unchecked.
**Read:** `app/src/engine/literalPrec.ts` (needsHuge, literalCounts, literalStart, literalPastCap); `app/src/engine/giacPrint.ts` (callsOf with its open and close, names, numbers); `app/src/engine/converge.ts` PERIODIC; `app/src/engine/decisions.ts` pastCapFloats (it reads literalStart's unchecked); `literalPrec.test.ts`; the g307, g307f1, g307f2 and g58-exp-1e7 rows of `tests/golden/precision.yaml`; decisions.md "G307 past the cap".

Guards: `sin(1)+pi^3000` and `pi^3000*cos(0)` start at P, checked (as before G307f2); `exp(10000000)`, `pi^3000*sqrt(2)` and `exp(i*pi^200)` unchanged; every g307, g307f1 and g307f2 row and g58-exp-1e7-12 and -30 keep their marks.

Find the calls with callsOf (open and close) and the huge powers' places with the number reader; read a base's sign with giacPrint's readers (a helper there if one is missing: giacPrint is G105's one module).

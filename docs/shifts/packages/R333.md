### ron, R333: a drag that scrolls a long row to its end never slides the row, branch `p1/ron-rowscrollstop`
**Lane:** A, night-safe, Sonnet (one rule in a pure function, pinned by tests). **Starts when:** –. A fix of how a gesture deletes; before the freeze (owner, 2026-11-15: the queue holds everything).
**Why:** (backlog F237; A139's finding; decided in Opus pass 161, `docs/archive/decisions-2026-10.md:62`: "A long row's drag that scrolls it to its end stops there: only a new drag slides it") One fast drag on a history row whose input or result scrolls sideways first scrolls it to its end, then, in the same drag, slides the row out and deletes it. `share()` (`app/src/views/rowSwipe.ts:33-47`) passes the rest of a move to the row as soon as the scroller is at its end, and `move` (`:117-124`) applies it within the one drag; `up` (`:132-141`) then deletes past 40 % or on a fling. 1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1:**
   - vitest (`rowSwipe.test.ts`): a drag that moved the scroller (it started away from the end, or the scroller's position changed during the drag) leaves the row at offset 0 for every later move of that drag, in both directions; the next drag, started at the end, slides the row as today.
   - e2e: a history row with a long result (wider than the screen), one fast drag across from its start: the row is still there and its result is scrolled to the end; a second drag deletes it.

2. **The change:** the drag remembers whether it moved the scroller; once it has, `share()`'s rest past the scroller's end is dropped for the rest of that drag (the row stays at 0). Nothing else in the thresholds (`SWIPE_SHARE`, `FLING_*`) changes.

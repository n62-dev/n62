### gorge, G551: bitand, bitor and bitxor read a small negative integer as a signed one beside a big one, branch `p1/gorge-bitsigned`

Before the freeze, in lane A after R362 (a wrong exact value; the word keys of R191–R194 reach it); else a fix row before the tag (release-0.1.0.md line 35).
**Why:** (T2 bug hunt 2026-11-16, F302; `../drafts/2026-11-16-ultra/t2-bughunt/hunt-bases.md`, probes `probes/bases/`, engine `_out/gsl`, phase-1 `e93dec77`.)
- **What happens:** Home `bitand(-2,18446744073709551615)` (Math › bitand; the hex literal 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF types the same) shows `4294967294` with `=`. Right: 18446744073709551614 (Python `-2 & (2**64-1)`, `source: derived`).
- **More:** `bitor(-1,2^31)` shows 4294967295 (right −1). `bitxor(-1,2^63)` shows 9223372041149743103 (right −9223372036854775809). `bitand(-1,2^70)` shows 0 (right 2^70).
- **How wide:** a sweep of 24 values from ±0 to ±2^100, all pairs, against Python: 282 of 1752 are wrong. Every wrong one pairs a negative small integer (Giac `_INT_`, |v| < 2^31) with a big one (`_ZINT`, |v| ≥ 2^31). Pairs of two small or two big integers are right, and so is `bitnot`.
- **Cause (stock giac 2.1.0, as read by the hunt; recheck the lines):** `src/misc.cc` `giac_bitand` about 6154-6162, `giac_bitor` about 6177-6185, `giac_bitxor` about 6200-6208. Each has `case _INT___ZINT: mpz_set_ui(e->z,a.val)` and the mirrored `_ZINT__INT_` case. `mpz_set_ui` of a negative int gives 2^32 + v. Parisse's latest tree has the same lines.
- **Our side:** `app/src/engine/wordOps.ts:4-5` says these three "are right on big and negative integers and stay Giac's", and docs/04 5.4 repeats it. The golden row `g235-bitand-neg` (`tests/golden/home.yaml`) tests small pairs only. Our own shl, shr, rotl, rotr and bnot use none of the three and are right (checked at 64 bits, signed).

### ron, R703: the keypad, the status line and Mode fit at Android font scale 1.5 and 2.0 on a real WebView, branch `p1/ron-textzoom`
**Lane:** A, Opus (layout under the keypad's fit pass); the night's device or an emulator for the device step. **The owner's cut (2026-11-17):** v0.1.1, after the tag (owner, 2026-11-17) if not merged by the stop (release-0.1.0.md, the freeze row). **Starts when:** R700 merged (both touch `App.svelte`); after the stop also R722 and R782 (it fits the bar on the keypad; R782 fixes the slots' widths). It is a fix (F383; R41 promised it); no key moves.

**Why:**
- **F383 (T5, [findings.md](../../research/t5-ui/findings.md); the probe emulator, Pixel 7 profile, `settings put system font_scale`):**
  - **At 1.5:** "Deselect" runs 5 px past the screen edge, "Select" shows as "Selec" and "Settings" as "Setting", and Math and Tmpl run into their ▾.
  - **At 2.0:**
    - the cluster's and row 3's faces overlap their neighbours, and the Settings legend reads "Settir";
    - the status line is cut at its top;
    - Mode's labels show as "Nu…" and "Significan…";
    - the tips' "Got it" is below the screen with no sign that the cards scroll.
  - 1.3 fits.
- **Why the test passes:** `app/test/fontscale.spec.ts` (R41) passes on the desktop, because `app/test/helpers.ts:397-409` scales only the app's `--fs-*` tokens. Android's WebView applies the system scale as text zoom to every text after the CSS sizes, so the emulation misses what the phone draws.
- **The decision:** decisions.md "R41 at font scale 2.0" would change "if the device clips Mode or ☰". The device does now.
- **Who it hits:** larger system text is common among older engineers; Android offers 1.3 to 2.0 in Display size and text.

1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1:**
   - **Device (test build, `app/test-device/fontscale.device.ts`, run by a small script like `tools/core-device.sh`):** at `font_scale` 1.5 and 2.0, with the start and the tips dismissed, check each of these screens: Home, CAS, Shift armed, Alpha armed, Mode, Settings, ☰ Home menu, Apps. On each:
     - no face, legend or label is wider than its box (`scrollWidth`);
     - nothing lies outside 0..innerWidth;
     - the status line's chips are inside its box;
     - the tips' "Got it" can be reached and the card list shows that it scrolls.
     - At 1.3 the same passes on phase-1 (the control).
   - **e2e:** `fontscale.spec.ts`'s head comment says that the token emulation cannot see the WebView's text zoom, and that the device test is the gate.
2. **The change:** where the text does not fit at the WebView's zoom, the fit pass measures after the zoom (it reads the drawn box, not the token) and shrinks the face and legends down to their floor. Under the floor, a label is ellipsed with its full text kept for the screen reader. The status line keeps its height (docs/03 rule 9) and drops the digits chip's text size first. No key moves. `WebSettings.setTextZoom(100)` (ignoring the user's setting) is **not** the fix: the report says it was weighed and why not (accessibility).
3. **Docs (the same diff):** decisions.md "R41 at font scale 2.0" as built, with the device numbers; docs/05 3.7 (the fit rule under the system font).

### gorge, G782: a %, ‰, ppm or ppb chip in Home is its pure number (0.42 % is 0.0042), branch `p1/gorge-ratiochip`
**Lane:** B, Opus. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-21 night (decisions.md "Owner answers of 2026-11-21, night": G369, as recommended: a chip in one ratio unit is its pure number, as typed). **Starts when:** –. No lock change.

**Why:** G369 (merged `b9bda425`) made %, ‰, ppm and ppb dimensionless units of the unit view (`find/units.yaml:101`, `ratio: true`). A unit-view result in one of them, kept as a chip and used in Home, goes in as the number shown (0.42 for 0.42 %) and the row says "unit dropped: Home has no units" (`app/src/state/session.svelte.ts:491`, R61/R63; `History.svelte:273`). For a ratio the unit is a pure factor (0.01, 0.001, 1e-6, 1e-9), so dropping it changes the value by that factor: a wrong answer.

**The result:**
- A chip whose unit is exactly one ratio unit (%, ‰, ppm, ppb; one token, power 1) enters Home's and CAS's expression as its pure number: the value times the exact factor (`0.42 %` → `0.0042`, `35 ppm` → `35/10^6` exact or `3.5e-5` at the working precision, as the chip's value is exact or float). Its σ scales with it.
- Such a row does not say "unit dropped" (nothing was dropped); a chip in any other unit keeps today's rule and note.
- The chip on the line still shows "0.42 %"; Copy and the row's shown LaTeX show the chip as on the line (only the value fed to the engine changes).
- Ans after such a row is the pure number.

### ron, R866: the unit pad's prefix keys end in a slot line (G_), drawn by CSS, branch `p1/ron-prefixslot`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, Opus. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-22 (his bug list, point 9: "maybe an underline after the letter like: G_, M_"; P1 of `../drafts/2026-11-22-owner-bugs3/s2-find-prefix.html`). **Starts when:** at once. No lock change. If R729 is still queued, R729 keeps this rule (its geometry test then counts the slot line as part of the face).

**Why:**
- A prefix cell differs from its neighbours only by `--key-pfx` (`Keypad.svelte:583-586`).
  - Measured on the app (sandra's `s2-probe-tokens.spec.ts`; `s2-contrast.mjs` on `s2-tokens.log`; sofia recomputed it, `verify.md` S2 point 9): **1.03 to 1.41:1** against the unit cells on the number keys, **1.00 to 1.29:1** against the function keys, over every family, brightness and contrast.
  - N62 Dark, the Android default: `#1e4948` against `#194241`, 1.11:1.
  - Only High contrast adds an outline (`:587-590`).
- So the prefix m (on `d1`) and the metre m (on `d9`) look nearly alike (`s2-360-unit-dark.png`).
- `spec/unit-pad.yaml:32-33` and the comment at `Keypad.svelte:583` say they "never look alike". That is wrong.
- The slot's reading in words still catches a slip (`unit-pad.yaml:39-40`), so this costs speed and trust, not a wrong answer.
- A shape reads in every family, in Plain keys and for colour-blind eyes. The slot line says what a prefix does: a unit comes next.

**The look:**
- After the prefix's face (G, M, k, c, m, µ, n, p): a short line at the baseline, about 0.42 em wide and 2 px thick, in the face's colour at 80 % opacity, 0.08 em after the letter.
- Drawn by CSS (a pseudo-element of the face), so the cell's text, its spoken name and the tests stay "G". It is never the `_` character that Shift + ␣ types.
- Out of the flow, as R143's ▾ is (`Keypad.svelte:527-541`: absolute, at its static position, in the key's side padding). The face stays centred: shift it left by half the line, as `--mark-shift` does for ▾. `fit.ts` counts it, as it counts the ▾.
- The colour step stays. High contrast keeps its outline, and Plain keys keeps the line.
- Legends are unchanged: `T` on G, `h` on k, `f` on p, and the σ▾ on `d1`.

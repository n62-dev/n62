### ron, R742: zebra history rows (`--row-alt`) and a picked row with a full `--focus` edge and a start bar, branch `p1/ron-zebra`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, after the tag (owner, 2026-11-17), Sonnet (the theme generator and 24 combinations). **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-17 ("it's not clear what you're looking at"). **Starts when:** after R832 (before the tag; it took in R741) and R743 (all edit `views/History.svelte`; R743's red strip, before the tag, sits behind these rows).

**Why:**
- Today a row is told from the next only by a 1 px `--line` divider (`views/History.svelte:327-335`), the input sits at the far left and the result at the far right, so on a wide row the eye cannot tell which result belongs to which input.
- The picked row is `--pick` with a 2 px `--focus` outline drawn on the row's own edge (`:336-340`); the row has no padding, so the outline touches the glyphs (seen at 412 × 815; the shot 2-picked.png in the drafts folder's 2026-11-17-history/shots, outside the repo). `--pick` stands only 1.17:1 from `--bg` in N62 Light and 1.13:1 in Solarized Dark.

**The result** (picture: the drafts folder's 2026-11-17-history/history.html, outside the repo; values: `tools/probes/history-1117/probe-zebra2.mjs`):
- **Zebra:** rows at an odd index counted from the oldest (`at`, `:207`) get `data-alt` and the `--row-alt` ground; a new row never recolours the others, a delete recolours the rows after it.
- **Rows bleed** to the history's edges (`margin-inline: calc(-1 * var(--sp-3))`, `padding: 5px var(--sp-3)`), and the history's `gap` (10 px) goes, so the pitch is unchanged and no row moves. The divider (`.item + .item::before`) goes.
- **The picked row:** `--pick` ground, a 2 px `--focus` outline inside its edge (`outline-offset: -2px`) and a 6 px `--focus` bar at its start (`box-shadow: inset var(--sp-15) 0 0 var(--focus)`). This is the whole-row pick (`data-part="row"`, R832); a lit input or result keeps R832's look on `--row-alt` too. No radius (the band is full width).
- **`--row-alt` in `scripts/theme-gen.ts`:** derived last, in every combination: the first 0.5 % step from `--bg` toward `--text` at which `--bg` : `--row-alt` ≥ `ZEBRA` = 1.08. Then text, muted, ≈, ⚠ and error are held at their floor (4.5, or 7 in High contrast) on `--row-alt` as on `--bg` and `--pick`, and `--focus` at 3:1 on `--bg`, `--panel`, `--row-alt` and `--pick`.
- **Base values** (normal contrast, colour keys): N62 Dark `#112e2d`, N62 Light `#c1dddc` (its `--focus` moves `#368281` → `#347e7d`), B&W Dark `#0d0d0d`, B&W Light `#ebeded`, Solarized Dark `#33455a`, Solarized Light `#f4f6cf`. In these six no mark colour moves: the lowest mark on `--row-alt` is 4.86:1 (⚠, Solarized Dark).

### ron, R938: a fold or unfold by swipe or ⌃ brings the newest history row back; a picked row stays (F496), branch `p1/ron-foldnewest`
**Lane:** A, Sonnet. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-28, his bug list 5 point 1 ("on swipe up the history page stays, but the user expects the newest entry to be at the bottom again"), and on scope: "maybe somebody wanted to stay at a history row to then insert; I would be cautious; I only want the slide up and down to handle it better, and on Enter makes sense as well". **Starts when:** after R937. **Lock:** none.

**Why** (sandra 2026-11-28, phase-1 685b38bc, sofia confirmed on 3981de9c; probe `../drafts/2026-11-28-owner-bugs5/probes/zz-scrollprobe.spec.ts`, outside the repo; 412 × 915, 25 rows): fold, scroll to top 200, unfold by ⌃, by a swipe up on the line or by Esc: top stays 200, **1008 px from the bottom**, the newest row out of view; a digit key after it changes nothing; only Enter scrolls (it adds a row). With no scroll the browser keeps the bottom by itself.
- The only scroll to the newest row is `views/History.svelte:174-180` (on the row count, the last row's status, the undo note); `unfold()` (`App.svelte:345-349`) only sets `folded = false` (Back `:362-364`, `onLine` `:352-354` the same); `<History>` (`App.svelte:451-472`) gets nothing about the fold; the pick effect (`History.svelte:182-187`) scrolls the picked row only.

**The result:**
- **A fold or an unfold by the swipe (down or up on the line) or by ⌃** with no row picked scrolls the history to the newest row (after `tick()`, `scrollTop = scrollHeight`).
- **With a row picked**, the same acts keep the picked row in view (the pick effect's scroll), so a user can scroll up, pick a row and insert it.
- **Not changed:** Enter (scrolls as today); typing with the history scrolled up (no scroll); Esc, Android Back, a tap on the line, `onLine` and Store ▶'s fold (R740) (no new scroll).
- History is told the cause, not only `folded` (sofia: a bare `folded` prop would scroll on Esc and Back too): App passes a counter or event that only the swipe and ⌃ raise.

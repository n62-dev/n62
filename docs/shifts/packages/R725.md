**The result:**
- **The sheet:** `chars:` in `spec/keyboard.yaml`, a palette like `symbols:` (:591), drawn by `paletteItems` (R18's grid), opened by `special chars`.
- **Layout:** 10 columns.
  - Each cell shows its digit (1–9, 0) in the corner.
  - A digit picks that cell in the row with the focus; ▲ ▼ move the focus between rows; a tap picks any cell.
  - The sheet rises over the view, as letter sheets do (App.svelte:540-545), so the digit keys stay uncovered (R21).
- **Cells, in rows:**
  1. α β γ δ ε ζ η θ λ μ
  2. ν ξ ρ σ τ υ φ χ ψ ω
  3. Γ Δ Θ Λ Ξ Π Σ Φ Ψ Ω
  4. ° ′ ″ ± × · ∠ √ ∞ ‰
  5. ≈ ≠ ≤ ≥ µ Å
  - Greek capitals that look Latin (Α Β Ε …) are left out.
- **Each cell has a `sample: [latex, giac]`**, or `refused: true` with the parser's message (`roundtrip.test.ts:65-78`):
  - Greek letters are names, spelt as Giac needs them (`beta_`, `gamma_`, `epsilon_`, as in `symbols:`).
  - Π is not π: it is a name; π stays on Shift + 3.
  - ° ′ ″ ± ∞ √ ≠ ≤ ≥ insert what `symbols:`, `relations:` and the keys already insert.
  - × and · are multiplication.
  - ∠ inserts what ∡ (Shift + ×) inserts.
  - ≈ is refused ("has no meaning"; ≈ is Shift + Enter).
  - Å is refused, as in `symbols:` (:602).
  - µ is the micro sign: in the unit view it is the prefix µ; elsewhere it is refused with a message that points to μ. Ω on a maths line is the name Omega; in the unit view it is the unit ohm.
  - ‰ is per mille, read as % is (owner, 2026-11-17): 5‰ = 0.005. `± n ‰` is refused.
- **Text only:** in a name or text prompt (`shell.entryLabel`: Store's name, Your units, a preset's name) a cell types its character, or its Latin spelling where the name rules need one. A refused cell types nothing on a maths line, and the notice gives the message.

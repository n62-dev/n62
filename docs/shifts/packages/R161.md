### ron, R161: the pressed key is our own flag, lit at once and at least 80 ms, never CSS `:active`, branch `p1/ron-pressflag`
**Lane:** A, night (before v0.1.0), Opus (the keypad's pointer handlers: a slip loses or reorders key presses, P17). **Starts when:** R151.
**Why:** (F191, owner, 2026-11-07, measured on the Fairphone in Brave 156 with a recorder on the pad's pointer events)
- Fast typing: the press look stays on one key and does not follow the next presses, and the keypad feels laggy. Every press reached the app (handler about 8 ms after the touch, the digit drawn at about 27 ms, no long task); only the press look was wrong.
- The press look is `.key:active::after` (`app/src/keyboard/Keypad.svelte` about line 437; the zones' opacity about line 542). On a touch screen Chrome sets `:active` from Android's tap detector, not from our pointer events: one and two frames after the touch no key had it (even when typing slowly); below about 200 ms between taps it showed the key before; then it stuck on one key for about 25 presses (3 s), with one finger (no overlap).
- A flag set in `down()` and cleared on lift showed the right key in the first frame for all 58 presses of the retest, 32 of them under 200 ms apart. The owner wants it lit at least 80 ms, so a quick tap is seen.
**Read:** `app/src/keyboard/Keypad.svelte` (`down`, `up`, `finish`, `cancel`, `cancelled`, `cancelAll`, the press CSS); `app/src/keyboard/gestures.ts`; `app/src/keypadBench.ts` (`tap()`, a pointer pair on a key); `app/test/` specs that tap keys (grep `pointerType: "touch"`); docs/05 3.10 (the press, R12c).
1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1:** an e2e with touch pointers on the pad: after a key's pointerdown, that key has `data-down` before the next frame and no other key has it; after its pointerup it keeps it until at least 80 ms after the down, then loses it; 20 taps 60 ms apart light each key in turn and leave none lit 120 ms after the last; a second finger's down lights its key while the first may still show its 80 ms; a pointercancel and the page hidden clear every flag; a trackpad zone gets the flag (its opacity), the hub none; ⌫'s repeat keeps ⌫ lit while held. A static test: `Keypad.svelte`'s CSS has no `:active`.
2. **The change:** one small piece in Keypad.svelte (no Svelte state, no re-render): on finger-down `data-down` on the touched key's element, on lift or cancel removed at the later of the lift and 80 ms after the down (one timer per key; a new down on the same key keeps it lit). Every `:active` press rule becomes `[data-down]`. The keys keep `touch-action: none` and the one pad handler.
3. **Docs (exact text in the report):** docs/05 3.10's press: lit from finger-down, at least 80 ms, by the app, not the browser.
**Done when:** the tests pass and failed on phase-1; the keyboard and gesture unit tests, the core list and `npm run gate` are green; the start chunk's delta is in the report (pin 61,000 B). **Owner's eye** (the next milestone review): fast two-thumb typing on the Fairphone, the press follows every key.

**Why:**

Help is not a pad cell (`app/src/units/pad.ts:159`), so it reaches `unitPad.other` (`units/open.ts:236-258`), which has no `help` case and ends in `c2.dead()` (`:256`). Evidence: sandra's sweep (`../drafts/2026-11-22-owner-bugs3/s3.md` point 11), confirmed by sofia's rerun (`verify.md` S3-11).
- The reading names the slot in words (`units/slot.ts:424-452`, "kilometer per hour") but not its size. The size is what decides between look-alikes: ton (short) or l ton, ft or ft_US. The index has every factor exactly (`si`, `p/q` with pi), and `units/slot.ts:81-130` (`exactFactor`) reads it as {n, d, pi}.

**The result:** the Help action in the unit view, with no sheet open, opens a sheet titled "Help: <the slot as `slotText` writes it>" over the view, using R838's path.
- **A slot with a unit:**
  - One row per factor, from R869's card value line: "km · kilometre: 1 km = 1000 m", "h · hour: 1 h = 3600 s", each with its hint ("exact", or "≈" with the fraction).
  - A prefixed pair that Find does not hold (the reading marks it rare) shows the prefix's row and the unit's row.
  - Last, the whole slot in SI, the exact product of the factors with their powers: "1 km/h = 5/18 m/s ≈ 0.277777777778 m/s". It is "=" alone when the decimal ends within 30 digits. Powers of pi are carried, and an offset unit alone gives R869's offset line.
- **An incomplete slot** (a prefix or √ armed alone): the armed prefix's row only.
- **An empty slot:** the units tip's text (`tips.units`, `i18n/enTips.ts:12`). On the empty end slot, the reading's own line (`units.read.endEmpty`) then the tip.
- Esc, Back, × or a second Help close the sheet onto the view unchanged: slots, active slot, caret, armed prefix, result. The focus returns to the view. While the sheet is open, keys go to the sheet (R838).
- Help in the unit view's Find is R869's, unchanged.

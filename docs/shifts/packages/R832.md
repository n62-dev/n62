### ron, R832: a tap on a row's input lights the input, on its result the result, on the rest of the row the whole row; Copy, Store and insert act on what is lit, branch `p1/ron-rowpart`
**Lane:** before the tag (the main queue), Sonnet. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-20 night, his H2. It takes in R741 (a tap anywhere picks; folded in by the coordinator, 2026-11-20, its tests listed below) and rewrites the whole-row pick of R147, R276 and R740 to R744 where a half is tapped. **Starts when:** after R830 (both edit `clipboard/actions.svelte.ts`). **Lock:** no list changes; docs/06 3b's "History rows at the tag" line is rewritten (a gesture, not in the lock file).

**Why:**
- Either half picks the whole row (`views/History.svelte:206-262`, `:345-349`), and Store ▶, Copy › and Shift + Select take the result even when the input was tapped (sandra's probe, `../drafts/2026-11-20-owner-bugs2/H/h-rows.spec.ts`, outside the repo). The "Copied" notice does not say what (`actions.svelte.ts:85`).
- The rest of the row picks nothing; the buttons are 7 % of the row (R741's measure).

**The result:**
- **A tap on the input** lights the input only (`data-part="input"`: `--pick` ground and a 2 px `--focus` outline on its box). **A tap on the result** lights the result only (`data-part="result"`). **A tap anywhere else on the row** (the gap, the edge, the mark, a uses or σ line, an error's message) or **▲ from an empty line** picks the whole row, drawn as today (`data-part="row"`).
- **A second tap:** on the lit part it does that part's job as today (input recalls, result inserts, R147; the stat rows open their sheet, R184); on the other part it lights that part; on the rest of a part-lit row it picks the whole row; on the rest of a whole-picked row it drops the pick (R741).
- **What acts on what is lit:**
  - result or row: Store ▶, Copy ›, Shift + Select and Enter take the result, as now; ◀ recalls the input.
  - input: the bar's `Copy` (no ›; the input has one text) and Shift + Select copy the input's plain text (what a hold on it copies); Enter puts the input at the caret (on an empty line: the recall); Store ▶ recalls the input and adds ▶, so the line reads `2+3▶` and the Store sheet opens.
  - Delete (☰ 1, a swipe) and Share act on the entire row, whatever is lit.
- **The notice names the copy:** "Copied: 0.333 333 333 333" (one line) for the bar, Shift + Select and a hold.
- **Unchanged:** a hold copies the half under the finger; a swipe left deletes the row and a swipe or a drag never lights a part; the reason line (R124) and Cancel keep their own taps; a running row can be picked, a note row cannot; while the keypad is folded a tap picks the whole row as today (the owner: "folded rows unchanged").
- **A σ row (R40):** the tap that picks (any part) also shows the σ line; the tap that drops the pick hides it.

### ron, R884: Settings › Numbers › Uncertainty gains "Rounded to σ", branch `p1/ron-uncround`
**Lane:** A, Opus. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-23: "both are needed and the user can decide, the default is we show them greyed out as we do for other instances! then the enabled option would round the digits to that specific precision, which an engineer might accept for the convenience". **Starts when:** R883 and R885 are merged (R885 sets the one σ drawing rule; this package rounds through its function).
**Why:** a value with σ shows every digit the digits setting asks for, with the ones past σ muted (R40, R883). An engineer reporting a measurement writes it rounded to its uncertainty, as a lab report does (σ to two significant digits, the value to σ's last digit): `9.811 ± 0.053 m/s²`, not `9.81065219207 ± 0.053`. The unit view's headline already writes this form (σ at 2 significant digits, `units/types.ts:185`, docs/04 2.6). The history row and the other places do not.
**The setting:** Settings › Numbers › Uncertainty, a third choice after the two of R40:
- "Muted digits, σ on tap" (default, unchanged);
- "Muted digits" (unchanged);
- **"Rounded to σ, σ on tap"**: every value with a σ is drawn rounded to σ's last shown digit, σ at 2 significant digits, and a tap shows the σ line as with the default.
It applies to every value with a σ, wherever it comes from: a constant's CODATA uncertainty, a typed ±, a stored name and a chip that carry one. It covers the Home history row, the unit view's result lines and the σ line.

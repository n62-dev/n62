### gorge, G522: the golden gate solves with units: a `solveunits` stage and a scale sweep over `solve.yaml`, branch `p1/gorge-solveunitsgate`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Sonnet (a gate stage over G500's builder; no app code). **Starts when:** G500 merged; after the v0.1.0 tag (0.2). Before R521 merges, so Solve's units are gated from their first caller.

**Why:**
- **G500's builder is tested in vitest only.** The golden gate runs `solve.yaml` through the solver (`app/scripts/golden.ts:86`, `golden/cases.ts:39` `solveCase`) and `units.yaml` through the unit view (`golden/units.ts`), but nothing runs an equation with units end to end, on every engine or parser change, at every digits setting.
- **The risk is the scale** ([S45](../../research/r-solveunits.md) section 4): the root test's floor of 1 means "one of the user's units" only when the builder divides by the right unit. A wrong factor gives right-looking digits with a blind ⚠. A sweep that moves every existing Solve case into units, and checks it lands on the same digits times an exact factor, catches it.

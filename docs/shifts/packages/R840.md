### ron, R840: every window that pops up gets a 2 px edge in its theme's accent (`--sheet-edge`), branch `p1/ron-sheetedge`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, Sonnet. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-20 night, his P1 ("take the theme's colour that stands out"; earlier: "windows that pop open better distinguished by an orange border"); no mock-up asked. **Starts when:** after R770 (every window in the viewport). **Lock:** none (look only).

**Why:**
- A sheet's edge today is `var(--stroke-thin) solid var(--line)` (`app/src/views/Sheet.svelte:309`): `--line` stands 1.6–2.1:1 from the screen (sandra's measure, `../drafts/2026-11-20-owner-bugs2/P/p1-edge-contrast.py` and the `P1-now-*.png` shots, outside the repo), under the 3:1 a non-text edge needs. Since R770 every window opens over the history, so a sheet must be told from the rows behind it.

**The result:**
- **One generated token per family and brightness, `--sheet-edge`,** in `app/scripts/theme-gen.ts`, set last like R154's `--ring`: from the family's accent, the colour its Enter key wears (`key-enter`: N62 and Black & White the logo's teal #529997, Solarized its blue), its lightness moved (hue and saturation kept) until it stands **≥ 3:1 from `--bg` and from `--panel`**, and **≥ 4.5:1 in High contrast**. A family may name another role in its data (`edge`) if the owner later wants one; none does now.
- **Not Shift's orange:** orange is Shift's only (docs/05 3.10), and an orange frame would read as "Shift is on". The test checks the token's distance from `--key-shift` (the cvd distance `app/scripts/cvd.ts` keeps for Shift and Alpha) in every combination.
- **A window** = a sheet (every level), a menu (☰, Copy ›, Export ›), a popover (the key pop R162 excluded: it is a key, not a window; the notice toast excluded), the Find sheet, the matrix grid, the uncertainty and stats sheets. Each draws `2px solid var(--sheet-edge)` on its outer box; a full-screen window (R778's corner button) draws none (no edge to show).
- Inner lines (row dividers, the title rule, `Sheet.svelte:398,412,426`) keep `--line`.

**Read:** `app/scripts/theme-gen.ts:350-400` (`withRing`, the R154 pattern), `app/scripts/theme-families.ts`, `app/scripts/theme-gen.test.ts`, `app/scripts/cvd.ts`, `app/src/views/Sheet.svelte:285-335,700-720`, the popover components (`grep -rln 'popover\|role="menu"' app/src`), `app/src/style.test.ts`, docs/05 3.1 and 3.10.

### gorge, G554: exp of a float matrix is its series at the working precision, never the identity, branch `p1/gorge-matexpfloat`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Opus (a Giac patch; a wrong matrix with `=`). **Starts when:** –. Before the freeze if lane B has room after G553; else a fix row before the tag.
**Why:** (T2 bug hunt 2026-11-16, F305; `../drafts/2026-11-16-ultra/t2-bughunt/hunt-stats.md`, engine `_out/gsl`, phase-1 `e93dec77`.)
- **What happens:** Home `exp(approx([[1,2],[3,4]]))` shows `[[1,0],[0,1]]` with `=`. The e^x key on a matrix types `exp(A)`.
- **Also:** `B:=approx([[1,2],[3,4]])`, then `exp(B)` shows the identity with ≈. `exp(approx([[0,1],[-1,0]]))` gives the identity as well. CAS `exp(evalf([[1,2],[3,4]]))` gives the identity.
- **Right:** the exact `exp([[1,2],[3,4]])` already gives [[51.9689561987, 74.736564567],[112.104846851, 164.073803049]] (`source: oracle`, mpmath `expm`, 40 digits). expm([[0,1],[−1,0]]) = [[cos 1, sin 1],[−sin 1, cos 1]].
- **Cause (stock giac 2.1.0, as read by the hunt; recheck):**
  - `usual.cc` about 930-939, `numeric_matrix_exp`, reached from `exp` (about 984-986) when the matrix is fully numeric.
  - The loop test is `linfnorm(eee,contextptr)._DOUBLE_val > eps`. At Digits ≥ 15 the norm is an MPFR `_REAL`, and `._DOUBLE_val` reads the union's other bits as a tiny double. So the series never runs and `midn(n)` (the identity) comes back.
  - Even when it runs, `gen(1/j)` is a double, so the series has double precision at best while Home claims 40 digits.
  - Raw Giac: the identity at `Digits:=15`, the right numbers at 14.
- **Why N62 misses it:** Home's two-precision check sees the identity at P, P+20 and P+40.
- **Where it works:** `cos(approx(…))` takes Giac's analytic path and is right.

**Stored data:** none.
**Read:** line numbers as of `e93dec77`; recheck them at the start.

2. **Tests first, failing on phase-1** (`home.yaml`, `source: oracle`, mpmath `expm` at 40 digits):
   - `exp(approx([[1,2],[3,4]]))` → [[51.9689561987,74.736564567],[112.104846851,164.073803049]] ≈
   - `exp(approx([[0,1],[-1,0]]))` → [[0.540302305868,0.841470984808],[−0.841470984808,0.540302305868]] ≈
   - A canary at 40 digits: element [0,0] of `exp(evalf([[1,2],[3,4]]))` at `Digits:=40` against 51.96895619870500365812448424264742062785 to 38 digits.
   - **Controls** (unchanged): the exact `exp([[1,2],[3,4]])`; `exp(approx([[0,0],[0,0]]))` = identity; `cos(approx([[1,2],[3,4]]))`.
3. **The change:** one patch at the cause (P19). The loop compares with `is_greater(linfnorm(eee),eps)`, and the term's factor is `inv(gen(j))` (exact), so each term keeps the working precision; eps follows Digits. If that is not enough at Digits 40 (the canary says so), `numeric_matrix_exp` is skipped above Digits 14 in favour of `analytic_apply`. Send it to Parisse.

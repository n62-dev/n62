### ron, R867: a sideways drag on the round pad steps over a whole number or name; taps stay one character, branch `p1/ron-padtoken`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, Opus. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-22, point 14 ("we scroll past every digit … per number, variable"): a sideways drag steps per number or name, taps stay one character, Ctrl+arrow the same; this changes P7 (1). **Starts when:** at once.

**Why** (sandra's probe `s5-probe-drag.spec.ts`, rerun by sofia, `../drafts/2026-11-22-owner-bugs3/`; phase-1 d4fe4c06):
- A pad drag makes one ◀ or ▶ press per 12 px (`keyboard/gestures.ts:42`, `Keypad.svelte:324`, `:342-344`, marked `drag: true`). Each press moves one atom, and `settle` (`entry/stops.ts:68`, `field.ts:334`) skips no digit. Crossing `0.98934893284` takes 13 steps, 156 px. A stroke has about 240 px of room (the arrow keys sit at x 140–272 of 412).
- MathLive's `moveToNextWord` is not usable as it is: it stops inside a unit chip (offsets 6 and 8 of `9.81 m+1`), between sin and its bracket (offset 1), and skips a power's whole exponent (`2^{10}`: 0 → 5).
- A hardware Ctrl+→ today jumps to the line's end (0 → 15). It is MathLive's own binding: `hwkeys.ts:30` leaves chords the table does not name to MathLive.

**The rule:**
- After a drag step left or right, the cursor keeps going while it is strictly inside a token. A token is:
  - a run of digits, points and decimal commas (`numberRun.ts` PART);
  - the ᴇ mark with the sign after it;
  - a run of single letters on one level (`abc`).
- So `6.022ᴇ-23` takes three steps: the mantissa, `ᴇ-`, then `23`.
- As today:
  - each box of a template is entered: fraction, power, root, matrix cell;
  - a chip, a function name with its bracket, and an operator are one step each;
  - up and down drags do not change;
  - ◀ ▶ taps, hardware arrows and a tap on the line move one character.
- Ctrl+← / → on a hardware keyboard takes the same step as a drag.

### gorge, G620: a limit of sinh, cosh or tanh at a finite point is their value, never Giac's infinity, branch `p1/gorge-hyplimit`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Opus (Giac's limit; a wrong `infinity` from the lim template, the derivative's own definition). **Starts when:** –. **Before the freeze** if a slot is free (an everyday calculus input); else the first fix row before the tag.
**Why:** (T6b bug hunt 2026-11-16, F434; a sympy-checked sweep of 80 random limits at 0, `../drafts/2026-11-16-ultra/t6b/probes/genlim.py`, `lim-r5.out`; `cas.mts`, `tools/probes/engine.ts`; engine `_out/gsl`, phase-1 `418f125e`.)
- **What happens:** the lim template `\lim_{x\to2}\frac{\sinh(x)-\sinh(2)}{x-2}` (Giac text `limit((sinh(x)-sinh(2))/(x-2),x,2)`) shows `infinity`, unmarked, in CAS and Home. Right cosh 2 = 3.76219569108 (`source: derived`: the derivative of sinh). The same `infinity`, raw Giac too:
  - `limit((cosh(1+x)-cosh(1))/x,x,0)` (right sinh 1), also one-sided (`,1`, `,-1`), and with `h` for x
  - `limit((tanh(2+x)-tanh(2))/x,x,0)` (right 1/cosh²2), `limit((cosh(x)-cosh(1))/(x-1),x,1)`, `limit((1/cosh(1+x)-1/cosh(1))/x,x,0)`, `limit((cosh(1+x)^2-cosh(1)^2)/x,x,0)`
  - `limit((sinh(cos(x))-sinh(1))/x^2,x,0)` (right −cosh(1)/2), `limit((cosh(sin(x)+1)-cosh(1))/x,x,0)`
  - even `limit((cosh(1+x)-(exp(1)+exp(-1))/2)/x,x,0)`
- **Right today:** the same with exp, sin, cos, ln, sqrt, atan, asinh, atanh (`limit((exp(1+x)-exp(1))/x,x,0)` = e); with a free name (`limit((cosh(a+x)-cosh(a))/x,x,0)` = sinh a); constants that cancel as text (`(cosh(1+x)-cosh(1-x))/x` = 2 sinh 1); and Giac's own `series`: `series((sinh(x)-sinh(2))/(x-2),x,2,2)` = `cosh(2)+sinh(2)/2*(x-2)+…`, `series((sinh(cos(x))-sinh(1))/x^2,x,0,2)` = `-cosh(1)/2+…`.
- **Cause (Giac 2.1.0 with our patches; `series.cc` `limit`, about 2340-2420 in `../drafts/2026-11-11-parisse/archive/giac-n62-2026-11-15/series.cc`; to pin down in step 1):** the hyperbolic functions are rewritten to exponentials only when their argument tends to ±∞ (about 2382-2388); at a finite point the leading term of `cosh(1+x)−cosh(1)` is not seen to be 0, so the quotient's leading term is a non-zero constant over x. `series` of the same expression finds sinh(1)·x.
- **Why N62 misses it:** converge.ts `check` refuses a limit only when Giac says `undef` or `bounded_function`, or the one-sided limits differ; `infinity` at a finite point is kept as Giac's answer.

### ron, R797: a hold in the unit view copies: a slot or the reading its unit's symbols, a message its text, the result its row's string, branch `p1/ron-unitholdcopy`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, Opus. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-20, his list item U7 ("long press on any text like in unit 'kilogram …' should copy it"). Then, on 2026-11-22, his point 10 ("tap and hold on the unit preview like m: meters should copy the whole unit string") and his answer to sandra's S3 question: **symbols, not words** (owner, 2026-11-22: symbols). **Starts when:** after R790 (merged). No lock change. Error rows in Home are AFTER-TAG B6 with R832 (which took in R741), not this package.

**Why:**
- A hold of 800 ms (mouse) or 1.2 s (a CDP touch) on the reading or the result copies nothing: no "Copied", and the clipboard keeps what it had. sandra's probes are `../drafts/2026-11-22-owner-bugs3/s3-probe.spec.ts` and `s3-probe-hold.spec.ts`; sofia confirmed them in `verify.md` S3-10. `app/src/units/panel.ts` has no hold handler. `apps/longpress.ts:9` (`longpress`, 500 ms) and `clipboard/actions.svelte.ts:142-185` (`copyRaw`, `named`, `holdCopy`) exist.
- The reading shows the active slot in **words** (`units/slot.ts:424-452`): `m` reads "meter", and `km/h` reads "kilometer per hour". Words are a reading aid that nobody pastes. The app's own unit text is `slotText` (`units/build.ts:338`) through `unitProduct` (`units/dims.ts:121-129`): `m`, `km/h`, `kg·m/s²`. A result row's Copy writes units in this form, and Paste reads it back (R325, `clipboard/pasteUnit.ts:1-8`).
- The reading has `user-select: auto` (probe; `panel.css:11` sets nothing). On the phone's WebView a long press on such text probably starts the system's selection of one word, hence the owner's "whole". This is (to verify): the device check below.
- `docs/05-ui-plan.md:138` says "A hold on a slot is R797's", but this package as first written covered no slot (sofia: CONFIRMED). This rewrite settles it: a hold on a slot copies that slot's symbols.

**The result:** a hold takes the row hold's 500 ms (decisions "Row gestures"). Each hold names what it copied in the notice, as a hold on a history row does (`named`, R832):
- **On the reading:** it copies the active slot's unit as `slotText` writes it (`km/h`, `kg·m/s²`), and the notice reads "Copied km/h".
- **On a slot's box in the formula:** it copies that slot's symbols. The slot is found as a tap finds it (`open.ts` `slotAt`, R801), and the active slot does not change.
- **No unit to copy:** an empty slot, or an incomplete one (a prefix or √ armed alone, a ÷ with nothing after it), gives a dead press with no clipboard write. On the empty end slot (→ Check on, empty) it is a dead press too.
- **On a message** (`unit-message`): it copies the message's text.
- **On the result** (`unit-result`): it copies the Home row's string, as a hold on that row does (`holdCopy(row, "result")`).
- A short tap does what it does today: R801's slot tap, the chips, Store ▶, → Check, and ← →.
- The panel (`.unitview`) gets `user-select: none; -webkit-touch-callout: none`, as `History.svelte:332-334` has, so the system's word selection never starts.

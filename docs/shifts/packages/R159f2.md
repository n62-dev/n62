### ron, R159f2: a touch that lands just beside a unit chip picks it again, branch `p1/ron-unitnearmiss`
**Lane:** A, first in the next run, Sonnet. **Starts when:** now (R159f1 is merged, 157650e0). This is the FIX of Opus pass 102 in the log; run it once, under this id.
**Why:** R159f1 moved the pick to pointerup. Pass 102 reproduced a regression in Chromium Pixel 7 emulation with a touch of radius 16 sent through the CDP call `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, 2 px outside the chip below, right and left of it (the chip is 24 px high). pointerdown and pointerup reach the chip, `elementFromPoint` at the raw touch point is not the chip, so no pick runs; touchend is cancelled, so the click that used to pick never comes. The result stays km/h and the press counter still adds one. b2d67744 picked m/s on all three points.
**Read:** `app/src/units/panel.ts`, the `chip` function (about lines 50 to 80); `app/test/unitresult.spec.ts`; the R159 note in `app/scripts/size-check.ts`.
1. **Tests first, failing on phase-1** (`app/test/unitresult.spec.ts`): the real CDP touch of radius 16 at 2 px below the m/s chip gives `data-unit` m/s and the press counter (`window.n62.session.taps.counts.presses`) up by exactly one. Add the same check for a centre tap: one pick, one press. The existing cases (the press without a click; the real tap on More… with no click on Find) stay green.
2. **The change** (`chip` in `app/src/units/panel.ts`): a flag `picked`, false at pointerdown, set true only where pointerup calls the pick. The touchend listener calls `preventDefault` only when `picked` and the event is cancelable, and then clears `picked`. The click listener picks when `e.detail === 0` or `picked` is false, and clears `picked` in every case. So a near miss keeps the browser's click, which lands on the chip with detail 1 and picks, and a mouse click after a pointerup pick does not pick twice.
3. **Size:** if the unit view measure passes `UNIT_VIEW_PIN_RAW` in `app/scripts/size-check.ts`, raise the pin to the measure plus 50 B with a reason note in the form of the R159 note, and change the Unit view chunks row of `docs/04-architecture.md` section 8.3 in the same commit.

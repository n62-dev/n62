### ron, R815: in Memory a tap, Enter or a hold copies the name's full value, and the Copy key copies the highlighted row, branch `p1/ron-memcopy`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, after the tag, Sonnet (the owner, 2026-11-20 evening, his walk point C6). **Starts when:** R813 and R814 (the delete has its own gesture and key before the tap stops deleting). No lock change.

**Why:**
- A tap and Enter on a Memory row delete the name (`app/src/find/names.svelte.ts:146-150`); after R813 and R814 that job has a swipe and a key. The owner wants the row to give its value.
- The row shows a preview of 12 digits (`UserName.preview`, `app/src/engine/defs.ts:150-160`); a copy of it would lose digits, σ and the unit (principle 3). The history's Full value path (docs/04-architecture.md:122-129) is the one to use: every confirmed digit up to P (40) for a number, `a+b*i`, `{…}` and `[[…]]` for lists and matrices, `value ± σ unit` for a value with a unit.
- The number keys already only highlight (R800).

**The result:**
- A tap on a name's row, Enter on the highlighted one, or a hold on it copies the stored value as the history's Full value copies a result: `1/3` stored gives the 40-digit text; a name stored from the unit view gives `v ± σ unit`; a list `{…}`; a function its definition as typed (`f(x)=x^2+1`). The "Copied" notice shows what was copied, as the history's does (R774: it moves no row).
- The Copy key (Shift + Select) copies the highlighted row the same way.
- Clear all keeps its two Enters; a tap on it arms it, a second clears (as now).
- R314's e2e step "tap `a`, Undo" becomes a swipe (R813) in the same diff.

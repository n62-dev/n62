- **The super jobs:**
  - ◀ ▶: in Home and CAS the next or previous block (R810's box move); in the unit view slot to slot.
  - ⌫: deletes the block left of the caret in one press (Select then ⌫ in one; Undo brings it back); in the unit view it empties the active slot.
  - U1–U6: a tap or a hold opens that key's setup sheet (Y1, R849), set or empty.
  - Esc, and Fmt under Shift: end the held mode.
- **The marks R850 draws:** each key with a super job gets `data-superjob`; ▲ ▼ are dead (`data-dead`, as an armed key with nothing lit); every other key keeps its face and its normal job (the held mode stays on).

### gorge, G518: the worst case min…max of a unit-view result over its typed ±: exact where proven, proven digits past a turning point, else refused with the reason, branch `p1/gorge-worstcase`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Opus (a range that misses the true worst case is a silent wrong limit). **Starts when:** G508 and G520 merged (MPFI in the engine, G519; the bounds' parser `app/src/engine/interval.ts`); after the v0.1.0 tag (0.3). R519 draws it.

**Why:**
- **The owner's list** (night review 2026-11-09, fh-3, `docs/archive/night-review-2026-11-09.md:73`): "no worst-case min…max for a ± chain". Today only σ: `pasupm(12.34,0.05)*{m}*pasupm(3.2,0.1)*{m}` gives `39.5 ± 1.2` m²; its worst case is 38.099 … 40.887 m² (derived).
- **The owner, 2026-11-16 (T1's questions 8 and 9, on sandra's advice):** each typed ± is a limit; the range is proven, not refused for an input used twice or a cos.
- **Exact by proof:** on the box value ± u, MPFI encloses each ∂f/∂xᵢ (Giac `diff`); a sign-definite enclosure proves the direction, and the two extreme corners, evaluated exactly, are the range. An input used twice is fine.
- **A turning point:** bisect the unproven input, up to 64 boxes; past that the outer bound (MPFI) is shown at only the digits its gap to the values reached allows, ends rounded outward.
- **Corners alone are never used:** they miss turning points (`tools/probes/worst-case.py`: cos(0° ± 10°) gives 0.98481 … 0.98481; load power's high end 0.52847, truth 0.53191). The naive interval is 2× too wide on a divider, 56× on load power.

**Allowed:** `+ − × ÷`, powers, `sqrt`, `abs`, `exp`, `ln`, `log10`, the six trigonometric and six hyperbolic functions and their inverses, `max`/`min` (G508's rewrite), slots, constants; an input used more than once.

**Refused, with words** (`units.worst.<why>`): `jump` (step functions, `pasmod`, piecewise, `when`: `pasuu`'s jump flag); `pole`, `domain` (a divisor's, `ln`'s, `sqrt`'s or `tan`'s argument interval reaching its edge); `notNarrowed` (gap above 1 % of the width after the budget); `chip` (until G523); `endSlot` (absolute temperature or level end). Integrals and user functions: `notHere`.

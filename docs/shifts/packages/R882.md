2. **The change.**
   - **Registry** (`views/hintRegistry.ts`, lazy). Each entry is `{ id, target (a data-hint name), gesture: swipe-down | swipe-up | swipe-left | drag-x | hold | flick-up | none, text, sub?, ms, then: "after" | "beside" }`. `after` means the action that triggered the hint runs when it ends; `beside` is a note that takes no press (R706's later). One entry here: `fold.swipe`, target the entry line, swipe-down, 1700 ms, `after`.
   - **Host** (`views/hintHost.ts`, `hints.css`, `i18n/enHints.ts`, its own lazy chunk):
     - the dim and the ringed hole come from the target's box, as `tipsHost.ts` draws its rings;
     - one keyframe set per gesture, and a hand of our own drawing (the mock-up's SVG);
     - the card is `role="status"`;
     - any pointerdown or key ends the hint and runs `after` at once, and that press is used up;
     - Back ends it (openLayer);
     - under reduced motion it shows a still pose for 2.5 s.
   - **Seen state:** kv key `hintsSeen`, a list of ids. The Fold chunk reads it when it loads. Until it is read, `hintDue` is false: a hint never delays a key.
   - **The hook:** the check lives in the Fold chunk, since ⌄ is wired only once Fold is loaded (`App.svelte:516`). App's ⌄ tap asks it, e.g. `foldTap(() => fold("fold.tap"))`. Only `fold.tap` shows the hint; `fold.swipe` marks it seen, and `fold.hw` neither. If a chunk fails to load, the fold happens at once.
   - **Rules:** one hint at a time; never over a sheet; at most one unasked hint per launch (the fold hint is asked for by a tap).
   - **Start chunk:** measure it. The added bytes must fit the room left; if not, take an equal cut and name it in the report. No pin raise.
   - **Settings:** the row `tips.row` (`aboutItems.ts:87`) becomes "Show tips and hints again". It shows the cards and clears `hintsSeen`.

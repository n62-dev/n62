### gorge, G553: floor, ceil and round decide at the working precision, never at Giac's absolute 1e-12, branch `p1/gorge-floorepsilon`
**Lane:** B, night-safe, Opus (a Giac patch; a wrong integer with `=`). **Starts when:** –. **Before the freeze**, in lane B after G557, G398 and G399 (owner, 2026-11-16): plain inputs give a wrong exact integer.
**Why:** (T2 bug hunt 2026-11-16, F304; `../drafts/2026-11-16-ultra/t2-bughunt/hunt-roots.md`, probes `probes/roots/` (`b8.txt`, `raw.mts`), engine `_out/gsl`, phase-1 `e93dec77`.)
- **What happens:** Home `ceil(log10(10^12+1))` (the digits-of-n formula) shows `= 12`; right 13. More, each `source: derived` with δ checked by mpmath at 60 digits:
  - `ceil(pi*10^-13)` → `= 0`, right 1
  - `ceil(1-cos(10^-6))` → `= 0`, right 1 (δ = 4.99999999999958E-13)
  - `floor(-pi*10^-13)` → `= 0`, right −1
  - `round(-2.5-pi*10^-13)` → `= -2`, right −3
  - `ceil(sqrt(10^24+1))` → `= 1E12`, right 1000000000001
  - CAS gives the same.
- **Cause (stock giac 2.1.0; recheck the lines in the pinned tarball):**
  - `_floor` (usual.cc about 7762-7764) returns `res-1` for a negative value only when `!is_zero(res-tmp)`.
  - `is_zero` of a `_REAL` or `_DOUBLE_` is `fabs(x) <= epsilon(contextptr)` (gen.cc about 9587, 9591): an absolute 1e-12 whatever Digits is.
  - `_ceil` is `-_floor(-x)` (about 7817), and round goes through floor.
  - So any value within 1e-12 above an integer, or a negative one within 1e-12 below, is taken as that integer, at Digits 12, 40 and 100 alike.
  - Proof: raw Giac after `epsilon:=1e-30` gives 1, −1, 13 and −1000000000000001.
- **Why N62 misses it:** floor, ceil and round are decision functions (`app/src/engine/decisions.ts:38`), and decideTwice (`decisions.ts:552`) runs them again at P+20. The snap is the same at both precisions, so the two runs agree on the wrong integer.
- **Controls right today:** `ceil(5+pi*10^-12)` = 6 (δ > 1e-12), `floor(3-pi*10^-13)` = 2, `sign(pi*10^-13)` = 1, trunc and frac.

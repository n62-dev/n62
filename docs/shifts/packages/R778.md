### ron, R778: a window that scrolls gets a small corner button that makes it fill the whole screen, branch `p1/ron-sheetfull`
**Lane:** the freeze's fix lane, Sonnet. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-20, before the tag ("a button in the bottom right corner, as unobtrusive as possible, that expands the window to fill the entire screen, so looking for items in the list is easier; only for windows without a fixed size, not Tmpl"; "touch usage for full screen is intended"). **Starts when:** after R770 merges (the sheets then all open in the viewport). No lock change.

**Why:** with R770 every sheet opens in the viewport (about 250 px at 360 × 740); a long list (Find, Vars, Units, Chars, a menu over 10 rows) shows few rows. A full-screen view makes a touch search easy.

**The result:**
- A sheet that has no fixed size (Find's Math, Vars and Units, Chars, and any list with more rows than fit its box) shows a small, quiet corner button at its bottom right (a 44 px touch target, a drawn mark in `--text-dim`, no fill). Tmpl, the Shift palettes and every other fixed-size sheet show none.
- A tap makes the sheet fill the whole screen, keypad area included; the same corner button, Back or Esc returns it to the viewport. While full, the keys are hidden: it is a touch view (R770's key picking does not apply). The sheet's own search field and rows work by touch as before.
- The state is per open sheet: closing a sheet resets it; the picked row stays in view across the switch.

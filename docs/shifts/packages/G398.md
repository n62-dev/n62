**Why:** (night review 2026-11-16, be-4, S1; confirmed by its verifier at `d4bf19bf0`, engine `_out/gsl` of 2026-11-15 23:23) Home RAD `ln(1/(1-sqrt(2)))` shows ≈ 0.88137358702 **−** 3.14159265359·i and `sqrt(1/(pi-4))` ≈ **−**1.07932761087·i, 39 confirmed digits; right 0.881373587020 **+** 3.14159265359·i and +1.07932761087·i (source: oracle, mpmath 50 digits, `tools/probes/review-1116/be/oracle2.out`). Cause (stock giac 2.1.0, read by the hunt): `usual.cc:473-474` in `ln` returns `-ln(feuille)` for an `at_inv` argument, `usual.cc:1346-1347` in `sqrt` returns `inv(sqrt(feuille))`; both identities fail on the negative real axis, where they give the conjugate, and the agreement check evaluates the same wrong text at every precision (family 4). log10 inherits ln. Patch 0013 restricted the same kind of rule for `ln(a^b)` (`usual.cc:489`). A copy of a right row can flip too: `(1/(pi-4))^0.5` is right but prints `sqrt(1/(pi-4))`, and that text typed back gives −1.079i (verifier, raw Giac).

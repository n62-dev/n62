### ron, R808: the unit slot's caret moves with the arrows, and ⌫ and the powers act at it, branch `p1/ron-unitcaretmove`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, after the tag, Sonnet (the owner, 2026-11-20 evening, his walk point C3). **Starts when:** R807. No lock change: the arrows keep their actions; inside the unit view ◀ ▶ walk factors before slots, a job of the view, not a new layer. A whole slot at a time is R853's buttons (owner, 2026-11-22: no Shift layer on the arrows).

**Why:** after R806 and R807 a slot has a caret that is drawn, but no key moves it; the owner wants to walk into a slot and fix one factor. Movement and ⌫ go together: a caret that moves while ⌫ still acts at the end would delete the wrong factor.

**The result:**
- ◀ ▶ move the caret one factor. At the numerator's end ▶ goes to the denominator's start and ◀ comes back. At a slot's edge the next press crosses to the slot before or after, as today: ◀ lands at that slot's end, ▶ at its start.
- ▲ in the denominator goes to the numerator's end; ▼ in the numerator goes to the denominator's end. ▼ in a slot without ÷ keeps today's job (into the result's chips, `units/view.ts` `down`); ▲ in a numerator keeps today's.
- ⌫ removes the whole factor left of the caret (its prefix and power with it). The same power key again on a factor that has that power takes the power off (m², x²: m; the owner, 2026-11-20).
- ⌫ at the denominator's start removes the ÷ when the denominator is empty; with units in it, a dead key and a message ("The denominator still has units"), so J/(kg·K) never becomes J·kg·K.
- R806's two open points (pass 256): ⌫ at the numerator's start, with factors after the caret, is a dead key (R806 leaves it a silent no-op); ÷ with the caret inside the numerator keeps every factor in the numerator and puts the caret at the new denominator's start, as R806 built it; a case each in `slot.caret.test.ts`.
- A move while a prefix or √ is armed is refused (the armed refusal, as `#leavable`); a power with nothing left of the caret in its part is refused with a message.

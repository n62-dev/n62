**Why:**
- Today only ◀ ▶ (and Enter, ⌫ on an empty slot) change the active slot: `app/src/units/view.ts:303-312` `move`, `:417` `#move`, `:427` `#leavable`. Nothing reads a pointer on the field: the prompts are locked (`units/open.ts:247-251` lockedValue, `readOnly`), and neither open.ts nor view.ts has a pointer handler.
- The comment at `units/open.ts:247` says MathLive "gives a tap on the field no focus"; the walk found that a tap moves the focus, and a tap outside the field leaves it off the view's element, so the hardware keys no longer reach the view (`keep`, open.ts:214, runs only on a press).
- Probe to rerun first: `../drafts/2026-11-20-owner-bugs2/W/unit-tap-probe.spec.ts` (outside the repo; copy it into `app/test/` for the run, do not commit it): it logs the active slot, the caret and the focused element after a tap on the second slot, a tap inside a filled slot, ◀ ▶, and a tap on a Home row while the view is up. Put its lines in the report.

**The result:**
- A tap on a slot (its box, empty or filled; the end slot while Check is on) makes it the active slot, as ◀ ▶ would reach it; the caret is at its end (today's only place). It is refused, with ◀ ▶'s refusal, when the active slot cannot be left (a prefix or √ armed, a ÷ with nothing after it: `#leavable`). A tap on the active slot changes nothing.
- After any tap while the view is up that opens no sheet (the field, the panel, a history row, the screen), the focus is back on the view's element, so the next hardware key goes to the view.
- The comment at open.ts:247 says what is true.

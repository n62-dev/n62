### ron, R862: ☰ in the unit view opens the view's tools menu, branch `p1/ron-unitmenu`
**Lane:** v0.1.1, Opus. **The owner's yes:** 2026-11-22 (his list point 2, "the menu (☰) button does nothing in unit view"; sandra's S3 recommendation accepted: ☰ opens the view's tools). **Starts when:** after R838 (sheets over the unit view). No lock change (no key, layer or list moves: ☰ stays on the bar).

**Why:**
- The bar's ☰ is a key press (`app/src/views/StatusLine.svelte:185`, `MENU_KEY`, special `menu`). While the unit view is up, every key goes through the unit pad first (`keyboard/route.ts:465`). ☰ is no pad cell (`units/pad.ts:159`), so it reaches the view's handler (`units/open.ts:236-258`). `menu` is not in that handler's `PLACES` (`:68`), so it falls to `c2.dead()` (`:256`): a dead press. sandra's probe and sofia's rerun agree: no sheet opens and the view stays (`../drafts/2026-11-22-owner-bugs3/s3.md`, `verify.md` S3-2).
- The written rule makes this a gap rather than a bug: "where a view has no menu the press is a dead one" (`docs/05-ui-plan.md:85`).
- The view already has a menu, hidden at the end of Find on an empty slot. The units menu's last two rows are Constants and Tools (R92: `units/menu.ts`, `units/tools.ts`). They come after the prefixes and 22 categories (`spec/units-menu.yaml`), which takes a scroll to reach. ☰ is the app's place for "this view's actions".

**The result:**
- ☰ in the unit view opens a sheet titled "Units" over the view, with Tools' six rows in `tools.ts`'s order: **Check against a unit** (the end slot), **SI form**, **alternatives**, **unit alone**, **number into Home**, **Store in**. Under them is **Constants ›**. Each row runs exactly what the units menu's row runs: the same objects from `unitTools(view.shown, …)` (`open.ts:91`) and the same `constant` pick (`open.ts:122-131`). A row whose place has nothing to act on stays disabled with its reason, as in Tools.
- A tool is ☰ then its digit: 2 presses.
- A second ☰, Esc or Back closes the sheet onto the view unchanged: slots, active slot, result, armed prefix (R838's close path). The title row keeps the ⚙ that every ☰ menu has.
- While the sheet is open, the keys go to the sheet (R838). Nothing reaches the pad.

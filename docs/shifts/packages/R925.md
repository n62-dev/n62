### ron, R925: `×10ⁿ` after a number takes no unit slot in the unit view, branch `p1/ron-tenpowslot`
**Lane:** A, Opus. **Starts when:** R918 (the same view). Wrong unit, not silent (the unit shown gives it away). **Found by:** sandra b4c (bug list 4, point 3), sofia CONFIRMED; evidence `../drafts/2026-11-23-owner-bugs4/c-report.md`.
**Why:** **F379:** pick the history row of `h`, Enter: the line is `6.62607015×10⁻³⁴` (digits). The units key puts a slot on the number and one on the 10: `6.62607015[u1]×10[u2]^{-34}`. m in u2 gives `= 6.626 070 15E-34 1/m³⁴`, i.e. (10 m)⁻³⁴. m in u1, or `6.62607015ᴇ-34 m`, is right.
**Cause:** the slot-before-a-power rule (`input/unitFormula.ts:50-53`, R28) treats the 10 of scientific notation as a base of its own; `numberEnd` (`:50-57`) stops before `\times10^{…}`. A slot after a power is a parse error in units mode today.
**Read:** `input/unitFormula.ts`, `input/parse.ts` (units mode), R365 (the same slot-placement family), R28.

A test that the slot reader and the units-mode parser agree on each case. e2e: the line `6.62607015×10⁻³⁴`, m in its slot, Enter: `= 6.626 070 15E-34 m`.
   **Controls (unchanged):** `2×10^x` with a variable exponent; the ᴇ form; `unitslots` specs; the core list.
